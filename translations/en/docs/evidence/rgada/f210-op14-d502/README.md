# RGADA, f. 210, op. 14, d. 502

A working collection of photocopies of folios from the razborny spisok (review list) of the Oryol dvoryane and deti boyarskie of 1620/21.

Archival title: «Разборный список дворян и детей боярских орлян» ("Review list of the dvoryane and deti boyarskie of Oryol"), 141 folios. The exact reference and dating are confirmed by the printed RGADA inventory. The names and folio numbers were initially established from the published name index:

- l. 67 — Василей Дмитреев Анпилогов (Vasiley Dmitreyev Anpilogov);
- l. 94 — Олексей Васильев Анфилохов (Oleksey Vasilyev Anfilokhov);
- l. 105 — Марка Карпов Онпилогов (Marka Karpov Onpilogov);
- l. 109 — Иван Васильев Анфилохов (Ivan Vasilyev Anfilokhov);
- l. 137 — Илья Иванов Онпилогов (Ilya Ivanov Onpilogov).

### The Complete Digital Set, 25 August 2026

The whole file has been checked through the open catalogue: 409 files in three parts — ll. 1–49, 50–101 and 102–141. Comparing the images and checksums made it possible to link the working copies already saved precisely to the frames of the complete set:

- **l. 67** — `ч. 2, лл. 50-101/210-14-502 (184).jpg`;
- **l. 94** — `ч. 2, лл. 50-101/210-14-502 (251).jpg`;
- **l. 94 v.** — `ч. 2, лл. 50-101/210-14-502 (252).jpg`;
- **l. 105** — `ч. 3, лл. 102-141/210-14-502 (298).jpg` (frame 297 shows the same folio in full);
- **l. 109** — `ч. 3, лл. 102-141/210-14-502 (312).jpg`;
- **l. 137** — `ч. 3, лл. 102-141/210-14-502 (395).jpg` (frame 396 repeats the lower part of the folio at a larger size).

The complete set has been used as a research working copy and has not been moved into the public part of the site. Only the target folios and locating fragments remain in the repository. The presence of the neighbouring frames confirms the sequence of folios, but does not by itself replace a literal transcription of the entries in question.

## Reading Status

All five family entries have been turned into genealogical Records, with an honest status of partial transcription where the service formula has not yet been read in full. The names are checked simultaneously against the original folio, the sequence of neighbouring entries and the full index. To complete the reading it is necessary to:

1. finish locating the lower boundary of the entry on l. 67; the entries on l. 94 v. and l. 109 have already been located;
2. save a separate crop of the whole line or block of the entry;
3. read the line in full, without filling in illegible parts by guesswork;
4. separately state rank, allowance, armament and other details, if they can actually be read.

### Visual Check of 21 August 2026

- **l. 67:** the entry is visible on a small separate sheet, but its formula and boundaries have not yet been read confidently enough for a literal transcription;
- **l. 94:** the image is dark and low in contrast; the name has been established from the index, not from a reliable reading of the whole line;
- **l. 109:** the text is easier to make out, but there are several entries on the folio, and the block in question has not yet been located with proven accuracy.

### Localisation of 25 August 2026

- **l. 67:** the beginning of Vasily Dmitriyevich's entry has been found in the lower part of frame 184; its full lower boundary has not yet been proved;
- **l. 94 v.:** Aleksey Vasilyevich's entry is the third separate block of frame 252, between Tomila Terekhov Teplov and Samson Dmitriyev Onakhin;
- **l. 109:** Ivan Vasilyevich's entry is the first complete separate block of frame 312, before Ivan Semyonov Chyupakhin and two Kozakovs.

Exact colour crops have been saved for all three places. Thus the earlier task of "finding the right line among the entries on the folio" has been solved for l. 94 v. and l. 109 and partly solved for l. 67. The next stage is the palaeographic transcription of the formulas for allowance, pomestye and armament.

Folios 105 and 137 were added on 29 August 2026 after their separate blocks had been located. The Record states the proved fact of the mention, while the unread allowance, armament and service formula are explicitly left as open tasks.

A repeat check of the complete file on 25 August 2026 found no reliably legible formula of a former town, transfer or origin for any of the three men. This is not proof that no such formula exists: the blocks in question still have to be located and read in full.
