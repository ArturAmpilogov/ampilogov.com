# P0003 — Yevdokiya Isidorovna Rykova

## What Is Known

The marriage entry of 1891 explicitly names Yevdokiya's maiden surname, Rykova,
and her origin in Matveyevka. She was 19 years old; if the age is accurate, the
approximate interval of her birth is from 8 October 1871 to 7 October 1872.

From 1892 to 1914 Yevdokiya is named as the mother of the eleven children found
by Grigory Andreyevich Ampilogov.

## The Rykov Line

The guarantor for the bride was Lavrenty Yakovlev Rykov, a peasant of
Matveyevka. The shared surname and his role on the bride's side make close
kinship a probable line of search, but the document does not call him her
brother or uncle.
