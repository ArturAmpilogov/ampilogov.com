# P0002 — Grigory Andreyevich Ampilogov

## What Is Known

On 7 October 1891 Grigory Andreyevich Ampilogov, a 19-year-old peasant of
Tymoshivka, entered his first marriage with Yevdokiya Isidorovna Rykova, a
19-year-old peasant woman of Matveyevka. His age gives an approximate interval
for Grigory's birth: from 8 October 1871 to 7 October 1872, if the age in the
entry is accurate.

The entries for the births of the children from 1892 to 1914 repeat the parents
as Grigory Andreyevich and Yevdokiya Isidorovna. The surname, meanwhile, is
recorded and indexed as `Анпилев`, `Анпилов`, `Анпилогов` and `Ампилогов`.

## Family and Confirmed Events

The marriage to Yevdokiya Isidorovna Rykova and eleven children are confirmed by
separate parish register entries. The children's births form a continuous family
chronology: Pelageya (1892), Yevfimy (1894), Agafiya (1896), Yelena (1897),
Feodor (1900), Aleksey (1903), Yekaterina (1905), Ioann (1907), Klavdiya (1909),
Vladimir (1910) and Valentina (1914). A death is documented for Feodor on 8 July
1905 and for Yekaterina on 9 October 1907. The date of Grigory's own death has
not yet been established.

## Current Research into the Parents

No direct entry of Grigory's birth for 1871–1872 has been found so far: this
interval is missing from the available registers of Tymoshivka. The main
candidate is Andrey Alekseyevich P1562, connected with Grigory's marriage
through Yevfimy Yerofeyevich Sorokin. An alternative candidate is Andrey
Yeliseyevich P90132. Neither of them is shown as the father until a document
states the kinship explicitly.

Further bearers of the surname series that matter for the side search are also
kept as separate public cards: Anna Semyonovna P99125, Paraskeva Andreyevna
P99133, two Varvara Fyodorovnas who have not been merged, P99035 and P99344, and
the documented candidate deaths of Ivan Grigoryevich P99329 and Vladimir
Grigoryevich P99079.

## Do Not Confuse

Grigory is not identified with [P0016](P0016-grigorii-andreevich-ampilogov-1866.md),
who was 18 years old at his marriage to Irina Naydenova in 1884. If the ages of
both grooms are recorded correctly, there is a difference of about six years
between them.
