## Sorting Rules

1. By the earliest confirmed year.
2. Within a year — by surname and first name.
3. If two mentions have not yet been identified as the same person, they get different IDs.

## Statuses

- `single-record` — one confirmed mention;
- `multi-record` — several documents confidently refer to the same person;
- `identity-review` — a possible merge with another person is being checked;
- `migration-evidence` — the person takes part in a proved route;
- `private` — the profile exists locally but is not published.
