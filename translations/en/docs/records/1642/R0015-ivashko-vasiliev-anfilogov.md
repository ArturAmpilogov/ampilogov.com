# R0015 — Ivashko Vasilyev syn Anfilogov, 1641/42

## Original spelling

> Ивашко Васильев сын Анфилогов.

> *Translation:* Ivashko, son of Vasily, Anfilogov.

## Transcription

Ивашко Васильев сын Анфилогов. The next line, with his service description, is visible in the image but has not yet been read confidently enough, so information on horse and weapon has not been entered in the record.

## Extracted facts

- name in the source: Ivashko, a diminutive form of the name Ivan;
- patronymic: Vasilyev syn ("son of Vasily");
- surname form: Anfilogov;
- recorded among the servicemen of Oryol;
- the entry is on fol. 388 of the Oryol estimate book (smetnaya kniga) of 7150.

## Service and muster context

The Oryol part of the file is the estimate book of 7150, compiled under the Oryol voivode **Ivan Stepanovich Islenyev**. The printed inventory names the responsible voivode, but not the scribe, and does not prove that Islenyev personally took down the information from Ivan Anfilogov.

This is a local account of the state of a service town, not a list of a separate army that had set out on campaign. The voivode's administration recorded the servicemen present and their readiness. No specific regiment, campaign assembly point or direction of service is named in this entry.

Ivan cannot be called a novik: the line contains no formula of first enrolment, no new pomestye or cash oklad and not the word «новик». He is recorded among the general body of Oryol servicemen. Moreover, the Oryol muster roll of 1626 already names Ivashko Vasilyev syn Onfilogov, and the list of 1627/28 a man of the same name and patronymic with an oklad of 150 chetverti. The full name, town and rare surname make the identification very likely, but the 1641/42 line itself contains no direct reference to the earlier documents.

Historically the book belongs to the first years after Oryol was restored in 1636 as a fortress of the southern frontier line. The account of 1641/42 showed what men the town had at its disposal under the continuing threat of Crimean and Nogai raids.

## What it proves

- the existence of Ivashko Vasilyev syn Anfilogov among the Oryol servicemen of 1641/42;
- the exact documentary surname form `Анфилогов`;
- the archival location of the entry: fol. 388.

## What it does not prove

- a confident reading of the next line on horse and weapon;
- Ivan's assignment to a particular campaign, regiment or assembly point;
- novik status or first enrolment;
- absolute identity with the Oryol man of the same name in the lists of the 1620s;
- kinship with other Anfilogovs without a separate statement of kinship.

## Reading notes

- the name and patronymic were checked against the manuscript and an independent name index;
- in the original the word order is the usual one for the 17th century: name, patronymic form, then surname — «Ивашко Васильев сын Анфилогов»;
- the service description has deliberately been left unread: it must not be replaced with the standard weaponry of neighbouring entries.
