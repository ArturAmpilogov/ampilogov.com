# R0012 — Ermak Pavlov syn Onpilogov, 1641/42

## Original spelling

> Ермак Павлов сын Онпилогов на мерине с рогатиною.

> *Translation:* Ermak, son of Pavel, Onpilogov, on a gelding, with a rogatina (boar spear).

## Full transcription

Ермак Павлов сын Онпилогов на мерине с рогатиною.

*Translation:* Ermak, son of Pavel, Onpilogov, on a gelding, with a rogatina.

This is the whole of the independent line. It names one man and at once describes how he appeared at the muster: mounted on a gelding and with a rogatina — a heavy spear with a broad head.

## Extracted facts

- name: Ermak;
- patronymic in the source: Pavlov syn ("son of Pavel");
- surname form: Onpilogov;
- a gelding is recorded at the muster;
- weapon: rogatina;
- the entry is in the Oryol estimate book (smetnaya kniga) of 7150.
