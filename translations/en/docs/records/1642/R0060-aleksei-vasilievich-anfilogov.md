# R0060 — Aleksei Vasilyevich Anfilogov, 1641/42

## How the name is recorded

The published name index renders the entry as follows:

> Анфилогов Алексей Васильев — л. 375.

> *Translation:* Anfilogov, Aleksei Vasilyev — fol. 375.

This is an index form rearranged into alphabetical order, not a literal transcription of the whole manuscript line. The saved spread confirms the right folio and the position of the entry in the file, but the service continuation of the line has not yet been read confidently enough to attribute a horse, weapon or oklad to Aleksei.

## What this document is

The folio belongs to the Oryol estimate book (smetnaya kniga) of 7150. The printed inventory calls it the book of the Oryol voivode **Ivan Stepanovich Islenyev**. This establishes who was responsible for the town's administration and the compilation of the estimate, but does not prove that Islenyev personally inspected Aleksei or wrote this particular line. The name of the immediate scribe is not established here.

The estimate book recorded the personnel present and the military readiness of a service town: men, horses, weapons, fortress equipment and stores. However, it is not a list of a campaign regiment that had already set out. Neither a further place of assembly nor a campaign direction has been established for Aleksei's line.

Oryol was restored in 1636 as a fortress of the southern frontier. The estimate of 1641/42 shows the town's service corporation a few years after its restoration, in a situation of constant readiness for Crimean and Nogai raids.

## One man in documents of 1620/21–1645

This entry links up with other mentions of Aleksei Vasilyevich:

- **1620/21** — index of a razbor (review) list: Oleksey Vasilyev Анфилохов (Anfilokhov);
- **1626** — two Mtsensk roll calls: Oleshka Vasilyev syn Onfilogov;
- **1627/28** — novik of the 1621/22 enrolment, pomestye oklad 200 chetverti;
- **1641/42** — Aleksei Vasilyev Anfilogov in the Oryol estimate book;
- **1645** — Aleksei Vasilyev syn Onfilogov in the Oryol oath to the new tsar.

Name, patronymic, rare surname stem and service town all match. About three years passed between the last two entries; the sources give no sign of a second Oryol man of the same name. So the 1645 mention has been merged into profile P0036. This is a high-confidence genealogical identification, although no folio contains a direct formula "the same as before".

In 1627/28 Aleksei did stand in the section of noviki, but he was a novik **of the 1621/22 enrolment**. By 1641/42 he already had some twenty years of service behind him. He cannot be called a novik on the basis of the estimate book.

## Changes in the surname form

In the sequence of records for one man the following forms are documented:

`Анфилохов → Онфилогов → Анфилогов → Онфилогов`.

It is especially valuable that the change of 1641/42–1645 happens almost without a time gap. This is not an evolution of the surname over many generations but variant spellings for one adult serviceman in neighbouring Oryol documents.

## What it proves

- the presence of Aleksei Vasilyevich Anfilogov among the Oryol servicemen of 1641/42;
- the form `Анфилогов` and the exact archival location — fol. 375;
- the continuation of his documented service after the Mtsensk musters of 1626.

## What it does not prove

- a literal reading of the whole manuscript line;
- horse, weapon, cash or pomestye oklad in 1641/42;
- the voivode Islenyev's personal part in inspecting Aleksei specifically;
- assignment to a particular regiment or campaign;
- kinship with Grigory and the other Vasilyevichi without checking the original of the 1625 extract.
