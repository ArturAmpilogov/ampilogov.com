# R0017 — Kuzma Karpov syn Anfilogov, 1641/42

## Original spelling

> Кузма Карпов сын Анфилогов […]

> *Translation:* Kuzma, son of Karp, Anfilogov […]

## Full transcription

The confidently readable name part: **Кузма Карпов сын Анфилогов** (Kuzma, son of Karp, Anfilogov). After the surname the line continues with a service description, but it cannot yet be rendered without risk of error. So horse, on-foot status, weapon and oklad have not been added to the Record.

## Extracted facts

- name in the document: Kuzma;
- patronymic: Karpov syn ("son of Karp");
- surname form: Anfilogov;
- recorded among the Oryol servicemen;
- archival location: fol. 393v.

## Service and muster context

This is the Oryol estimate book (smetnaya kniga) of 7150, compiled under the voivode **Ivan Stepanovich Islenyev**. The inventory links the whole Oryol part with him, but does not claim that the voivode personally inspected Kuzma or wrote the line himself. The name of the scribe of this line has not yet been established.

The estimate book was a town report on the men and military readiness of Oryol, not a marching list of an already formed campaign army. No specific regiment, further place of assembly or campaign direction is named in the part of the line that has been read. Nor has any sign of novik status been read: neither the word «новик» nor a formula of first enrolment appears in the confident part, but the unread continuation does not allow additional notes to be ruled out definitively.

Oryol was restored in 1636 as a fortress of the southern frontier. The count of 1641/42 belongs to the period when this line was being strengthened against the threat of Crimean and Nogai raids.

The basis for the type of document, its dating, the boundaries of the Oryol part and the voivode's name is the [card of the printed inventory of the file](../../../data/genealogy/sources/rgada/opisi/RGADA-210-OP4-OPIS-D260.json).

## Connection with the 1645 entry

In the oath book (krestoprivodnaya kniga) of 1645 the name **Kuzma Karpov syn Onfilogov** is published. The rare name, patronymic, town and close date all match, so the identification is very likely. The 1641/42 Record, however, records only its own event and does not turn a probable match into proven kinship.

## What it proves

- the existence of Kuzma Karpov syn Anfilogov among the Oryol servicemen of 1641/42;
- the documentary surname form `Анфилогов`;
- the exact archival folio of the entry.

## What it does not prove

- horse, weapon, oklad or on-foot status, until the end of the line is read;
- assignment to a campaign, regiment or separate place of assembly;
- novik status;
- kinship with other Anfilogovs without a direct formula in the source.
