# R0016 — Ofonasey Osipov syn Anfilogov, 1641/42

## Original spelling

> Офонасей Осипов сын Анфилогов пеш.

> *Translation:* Ofonasey, son of Osip, Anfilogov, on foot.

## Full transcription

Офонасей Осипов сын Анфилогов пеш.

*Translation:* Ofonasey, son of Osip, Anfilogov, on foot.

This is the whole of the independent line. The word `пеш` ("on foot") is written after the surname and refers to Ofonasey's condition at the service count.

## Extracted facts

- name in the source: Ofonasey, a historical form of the name Afanasy;
- patronymic: Osipov syn ("son of Osip");
- surname form: Anfilogov;
- recorded among the Oryol servicemen;
- marked as on foot;
- no separate weapon, horse or oklad is named in the line.

## Service and muster context

The Oryol part of the file is the estimate book (smetnaya kniga) of 7150, compiled under the Oryol voivode **Ivan Stepanovich Islenyev**. The inventory establishes the responsible voivode, but does not name the scribe of this line and does not prove that Islenyev personally inspected Ofonasey.

The estimate book served as a local account of the forces and condition of a frontier town. It is not a list of a separate army that had already set out on campaign. So the word `пеш` describes the way Ofonasey was counted for service at that moment; it does not prove that he never had a horse. No weapon is named in the line, but no conclusion that he had no weapon at all can be drawn from the source's silence either.

No specific regiment, place of further assembly or campaign direction is given here. There are also no signs of a novik: the word «новик», a formula of first enrolment and the assignment of a new pomestye or cash oklad are all absent. Ofonasey stands in the general list of Oryol servicemen present.

Historically the entry was made a few years after Oryol was restored in 1636 as a fortress of the southern frontier. The town administration counted its men and their readiness against the background of the continuing threat of Crimean and Nogai raids.

The basis for the type of document, its dating and the name of the responsible voivode is the
[card of the printed inventory of the file](../../../data/genealogy/sources/rgada/opisi/RGADA-210-OP4-OPIS-D260.json).

## What it proves

- the existence of Ofonasey Osipov syn Anfilogov among the Oryol servicemen of 1641/42;
- the exact documentary surname form `Анфилогов`;
- that in this count he was recorded as on foot;
- the archival location of the entry: fol. 392v.

## What it does not prove

- whether or not he had weapons beyond what this line states;
- that he never served mounted;
- assignment to a particular campaign, regiment or assembly point;
- novik status or first enrolment;
- the earlier caution regarding the Afonka of 1628/29 and 1640 has been lifted after rereading fol. 109: there Ofonko, the patronymic Osipov and the surname Onfilogov are named; the exact name formula matches the entry of 1641/42;
- identity with the Oryol reiter (cavalryman) of the same name in a file of 1698: the documents are 56 years apart;
- kinship with other Anfilogovs without a direct statement of kinship.

## Reading notes

- the name and patronymic were checked against the original and an independent name index;
- the word `пеш` is rendered literally, without adding weapons absent from the line;
- the modern form of the name is Afanasy, but the Record's title keeps the document's form `Офонасей` (Ofonasey).
