# R0061 — Ivashko Mikhailov syn Onpilogov, 1641/42

## Original spelling

> Ивашко Михайлов сын Онпилогов на мерине с пищалью, саблею.

> *Translation:* Ivashko, son of Mikhail, Onpilogov, on a gelding, with a pishchal (matchlock gun) and a sabre.

## Modern reading

Ivan Mikhailovich Onpilogov appeared at the Oryol muster mounted on a gelding, with a pishchal and a sabre. The entry takes up two lines at the top of the right-hand page of the spread.

## Names and meaning

- **Ivashko** is the usual scribal form of the name Ivan; it does not mean that we are dealing with a child. In the oath book of 1645 he is already recorded as «Иван» (Ivan).
- **Mikhailov syn** ("son of Mikhail") is not a surname but a direct reference to his father Mikhail. In this way a separate patronymic line appears in the Oryol surname group, not yet connected with the 16th-century branches.
- **Onpilogov** is a variant of the same surname series as «Анфилогов» (Anfilogov): this is the form the scribe used in 1642, whereas in 1645 the same man is called Anfilogov.
- **The gelding, pishchal and sabre** show that this is an entry from a military muster, not a simple listing of inhabitants.

## Why this is the same Ivan as in 1645

The rare full name, the surname group, Oryol and the close date all match. So the entry has been merged with P0051 with high confidence. There is no direct phrase "the same man" in the books, and this is recorded separately as a limit of the conclusion.

## What is not yet proven

The source does not give the patronymic of the father Mikhail, his pomestye or his earlier town. So Ivan cannot automatically be attached to any of the Anfilogov lines of 1594–1596, and no migration route can be drawn.
