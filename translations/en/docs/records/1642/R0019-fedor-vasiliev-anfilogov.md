# R0019 — Fedor Vasilyev Anfilogov, 1641/42

## Spelling in the published index

> Анфилогов Федор Васильев — л. 403 об.

> *Translation:* Anfilogov, Fedor Vasilyev — fol. 403v.

This is an index form, not a literal transcription of the whole manuscript line.

## Extracted facts

- the index names Fyodor, son of Vasily, Anfilogov;
- it assigns him to the Oryol part of the file;
- it gives an exact location: fol. 403v.

## Place among the Oryol Vasilyevichi

Fyodor appears in the index close in time to Ivan, Aleksei and Grigory Vasilyevichi Anfilogov. However, the research extract of 1625 names only Aleksei and Grigory among the sons of Vasily. Fyodor may have been a younger son not yet included in the extract, or the son of a different Vasily. Neither option can yet be turned into a kinship link.

The oath of 1645 names another Fyodor, but on the original folio his patronymic reads **Дмитреев** (Dmitreyev, "son of Dmitry") and the surname form **Анпилога** (Anpiloga). So the Fyodor Vasilyevich of the estimate book and the Fyodor Dmitriyevich of the oath book are kept as different people. The three-year gap between the documents does not cancel the direct discrepancy in the patronymics.

## Service and muster context

The folio belongs to the Oryol estimate book (smetnaya kniga) of 7150, compiled under the voivode **Ivan Stepanovich Islenyev**. This establishes administrative responsibility for the book, but not the identity of the immediate inspecting officer or the scribe of this line.

The estimate book reflected the state of the town and its servicemen. It is not in itself a list of an army already sent on campaign. The name index does not say whether Fyodor was mounted or on foot, with what weapon he appeared, what oklad he had, where he was to assemble next or whether he was a novik. These questions remain open until the original line is reliably located and read.

Oryol at this time was a recently restored fortress of the southern frontier; military accounting in the town was carried out against the threat of Crimean and Nogai raids.

## What it does not prove

- the literal spelling of the full line in the original;
- horse, weapon, oklad, campaign or novik status;
- identity with Fedor Dmitreyev syn Anpilog of 1645: the patronymics differ;
- proven brotherhood with the other Oryol Vasilyevichi.
