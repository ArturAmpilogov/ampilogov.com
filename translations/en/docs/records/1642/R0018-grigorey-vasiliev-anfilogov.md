# R0018 — Grigorey Vasilyev Anfilogov, 1641/42

## Spelling in the published index

> Анфилогов Григорей Васильев — л. 402.

> *Translation:* Anfilogov, Grigorey Vasilyev — fol. 402.

This is an index form, not a literal transcription of the whole manuscript line.

## Extracted facts

- the index names Grigory, son of Vasily, Anfilogov;
- it assigns him to the Oryol part of the file;
- it gives an exact location: fol. 402.

## Place among the Oryol Vasilyevichi

An earlier research extract of 1625 names in Bazdyrevo two sons of Vasily — Aleksei and Grigory. If the extract has been transmitted accurately, the Grigory in it fits well with the Grigory Vasilyevich Anfilogov of 1641/42: name, patronymic, district and surname group all match. But the original of the 1625 extract has not yet been brought into the project, so the kinship has not been entered as established.

Ivan and Aleksei Vasilyevichi are documented nearby in the same generation. Aleksei can be traced to the oath of 1645, Ivan from the Mtsensk musters of 1626 to the estimate book of 1641/42. Such a concentration of Vasilyevichi allows for a common family, but the same patronymic does not by itself prove they were brothers.

It is especially important not to confuse this Grigory with the man in the oath of 1645. In the photograph there the reading is **Григорей Дмитреев сын Анпилога** (Grigorey, son of Dmitry, Anpilog). Grigory Vasilyevich and Grigory Dmitriyevich are two different servicemen.

## Service and muster context

The folio belongs to the Oryol estimate book (smetnaya kniga) of 7150 under the voivode **Ivan Stepanovich Islenyev**. The inventory names the responsible voivode for the whole book, but does not say who personally checked Grigory or who wrote his line.

This is a town count of the servicemen present. A specific campaign, horse, weapon, oklad, further place of assembly and novik category are to be checked once Grigory's line has been located on the original folio.

Historically the count was made soon after Oryol was restored in 1636 as a fortress on the southern border of the Russian state.

## What it proves

- the presence of Grigorey Vasilyev Anfilogov in the published index to the file;
- the archival reference to fol. 402.

## What it does not prove

- the literal spelling of the full line in the original;
- horse, weapon, oklad, campaign or novik status;
- identity with Grigorey Dmitreyev syn Anpilog of 1645: the patronymics differ;
- proven brotherhood with Ivan, Aleksei or Fyodor Vasilyevichi;
- identity with Grigory, son of Vasily, of the 1625 extract, until its original has been checked.
