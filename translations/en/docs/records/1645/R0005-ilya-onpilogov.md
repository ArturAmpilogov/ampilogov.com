## What it proves

- in 1645 Иля Онпилогов (Ilya Onpilogov) is named in Userd among the regimental Cossacks;
- the document directly records the surname form `Онпилогов`.

## A new Oryol candidate

In the Oryol razbornyi spisok (review list) of 1620/1621, fol. 137, an **Ilya Ivanovich
Onpilogov** has been found. The entries are 24 years apart; the combination of the given name and the rare surname matches.
This makes possible the path of one serviceman from Oryol to the new fortress of
Userd, founded in 1637. However, the first roll of the 105 newly recruited Cossacks
of Userd in July 1637 does not name Ilya: it contains two other bearers of the
surname — Anton Sofonovich and Pyotr Gavrilovich Onpilogov. So Ilya may have joined
the garrison later, or he may be a different man. The 1645 line has no
patronymic, so the two are kept in two profiles, and no route is drawn
on the map.

## What it does not prove

- Ilya's kinship with the Kursk, Oryol or Stary Oskol bearers of the surname;
- identity with Ilya Ivanovich Onpilogov of Oryol in 1620/1621;
- his age, family, the size of his landholding or where he served before.
