# R0008 — Ivan Mikhaylov syn Anfilogov, 1645

## Original spelling

> Иван Михайлов сын Анфилогов.

> *Translation:* Ivan, son of Mikhail, Anfilogov.

## Full transcription

Иван Михайлов сын Анфилогов.

*Translation:* Ivan, son of Mikhail, Anfilogov.

The given name and surname are rendered without modern normalisation.

## Extracted facts

- given name in the document: Ivan;
- patronymic in its 17th-century form: Mikhaylov syn ("son of Mikhail");
- surname form: Anfilogov;
- place in the document: Oryol;
- position in the list: a separate line among the servicemen;
- document: the krestoprivodnaya kniga (book of the oath of allegiance) of the year 7153, that is, 1644–1645.

## What it proves

- in the Oryol part of the 1645 oath book, Ivan Mikhaylov syn Anfilogov is named on a separate line;
- the document directly records the surname form `Анфилогов`.

## What it does not prove

- Ivan's kinship with other Oryol, Kursk or Oskol bearers of the surname;
- the size of his pomestye (service estate), his family or his weapons.
