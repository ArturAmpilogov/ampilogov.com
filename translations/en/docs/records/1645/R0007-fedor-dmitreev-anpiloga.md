# R0007 — Fyodor Dmitreyev syn Anpiloga, 1645

## Original spelling

> Ѳедоръ Дмитреевъ сынъ Анпилога.

> *Translation:* Fyodor, son of Dmitry, Anpiloga.

## Full transcription

Ѳедоръ Дмитреевъ сынъ Анпилога.

*Translation:* Fyodor, son of Dmitry, Anpiloga.

The spelling of the line is kept without modern normalisation.

## Extracted facts

- given name in the document: Ѳедоръ;
- modern form of the given name: Fyodor;
- patronymic in its 17th-century form: Dmitreyev syn ("son of Dmitry");
- surname form in the document: Анпилога (Anpiloga);
- place in the document: Oryol;
- position in the list: a separate line among the servicemen;
- document: the krestoprivodnaya kniga (book of the oath of allegiance) of the year 7153, that is, 1644–1645.

## What it proves

- in the Oryol part of the 1645 oath book, Fyodor Dmitreyev syn Anpiloga is named on a separate line;
- the image directly records the surname form `Анпилога`.

## What it does not prove

- identity with Fyodor Vasilyevich Anfilogov from the name index of 1642;
- kinship with other Oryol bearers of the surname;
- the size of his pomestye (service estate), his family or his weapons.

## Reading notes

- the patronymic reads `Дмитреевъ`: the letterforms have been compared with the neighbouring line on the same folio;
- the form "Fyodor Dmitriyevich Anpilogov" is used only in the index of people, not as a literal quotation.
