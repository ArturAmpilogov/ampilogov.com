# R0026 — Ivashko Vasilyev syn Anfilogov, 4 October 1626

## Original spelling in the publication

> Ивашко Васильев сын Анфилогов.

> *Translation:* Ivashko, son of Vasily, Anfilogov.

## Extracted facts

- Ivan was reviewed again in Mtsensk on 4 October 1626;
- he had been on the sovereign's service **continuously from 1 July to 4 October**, «без езду» ("without leaving"), that is, without departing from service;
- the surname is now recorded as **Анфилогов** (Anfilogov), whereas on fol. 105 of the same file it was Онфилогов (Onfilogov).

## Why a second muster was held

A tsar's charter received on 3 October required the servicemen to be reviewed in person once more: to separate those who had arrived on time and served without absence from those who had left or arrived late, and to check information about the absent by questioning. The next day Buturlin and Izvolsky held a new muster.

Ivan is entered in the section of the second half, which remained on service from 1 July until its release on 4 October. This is not a simple copying of the July name but a second documentary event, confirming more than three months of continuous service.

## Service assignment and status

Service was performed with the voivodes' regiments at Mtsensk and kept the southern frontier ready against Tatar attacks. There is no order for Ivan to go to a separate campaign point. He is not called a novik here; the later list places him among the gorodovye (town servicemen).

The change Onfilogov → Anfilogov within one file shows fluctuation in chancery spelling, not a change of family.

## What it does not prove

- Ivan's participation in battle;
- any specific route beyond Mtsensk;
- that the two forms of the surname denote different people.
