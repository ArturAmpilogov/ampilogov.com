# R0027 — Oleshka Vasilyev syn Onfilogov, 4 October 1626

## Original spelling in the publication

> Олешка Васильев сын Онфилогов.

> *Translation:* Oleshka, son of Vasily, Onfilogov.

## Extracted facts

- Aleksei appeared at the muster again on 4 October 1626;
- he served in Mtsensk continuously from 1 July to 4 October, without leaving;
- the surname form **Онфилогов** (Onfilogov) matches the July entry on fol. 105.

## A second check on service

Under a tsar's charter the voivodes were to establish in person who had actually served all summer, who had left and who had arrived late. The muster was held by **Ivan Ivanovich Buturlin** and **Leonty Agafonovich Izvolsky** on 4 October.

The position of the name in the section of those who had been on service «без езду» ("without leaving") confirms not only Aleksei's formal enrolment but his continuous presence throughout the whole shift of the second half.

## Where the men were assembled, and was he a novik

Aleksei remained with the voivodes' regiments at Mtsensk for frontier readiness; no further campaign point is named. In this entry he is not marked as a novik, but the list of 1627/28 assigns him to the enrolment of 1621/22. So "novik" is his early service category, not a result of the October muster.
