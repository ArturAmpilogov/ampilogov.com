# R0024 — Oleshka Vasilyev syn Onfilogov, 2 July 1626

## Original spelling in the publication

> Олешка Васильев сын Онфилогов.

> *Translation:* Oleshka, son of Vasily, Onfilogov.

## Extracted facts

- Oleshka is a historical diminutive form of the name Aleksei;
- Aleksei belonged to the Oryol service corporation;
- the surname is recorded as **Онфилогов** (Onfilogov);
- he appeared in person in Mtsensk on 2 July 1626;
- the entry belongs to the second half of the servicemen, who replaced the first from 1 July.

## The muster and the military situation

The men were reviewed by the stolnik and voivode **Ivan Ivanovich Buturlin** and the voivode **Leonty Agafonovich Izvolsky**. Although the whole file opens with the date 17 April, the actual date of this appearance is 2 July.

The second half was to remain with the voivodes in the regiments at Mtsensk in place of the first half, which had been released home. The reason for heightened readiness was news of the Tatar danger in Elets, Novosil and Voronezh districts. The document speaks of scouting and the defence of the southern towns; no separate campaign direction is named for Aleksei.

## Novik

The July line itself does not use the word «новик» (novik). However, the later list of 1627/28 explicitly places Aleksei among the noviki enrolled in 1621/22, with an oklad of 200 chetverti. So by 1626 he had already been in service for about five years since his enrolment, and was not a novik of this particular Mtsensk muster.
