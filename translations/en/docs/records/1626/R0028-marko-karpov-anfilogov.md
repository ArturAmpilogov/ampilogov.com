# R0028 — Marko Karpov syn Anfilogov, 4 October 1626

## Original spelling in the publication

> Марко Карпов сын Анфилогов.

> *Translation:* Marko, son of Karp, Anfilogov.

## Extracted facts

- Mark was reviewed again in Mtsensk on 4 October 1626;
- he was continuously on service from 1 July to 4 October, «без езду» ("without leaving");
- the surname is recorded as **Анфилогов** (Anfilogov), unlike the July **Онфилогов** (Onfilogov).

## The reviewing officers and the meaning of the entry

The second muster under the tsar's charter was held by **Ivan Ivanovich Buturlin** and **Leonty Agafonovich Izvolsky**. They were to check actual attendance and separate those who had served without absence from those who had left or arrived late. Mark was placed in the section of those who served the whole time, so the October line documents about three months of continuous presence rather than mechanically repeating the July list.

## Military context and novik

The second half remained in the regiments at Mtsensk, ready to defend the southern districts against the Tatar threat. Mark's line contains no specific march or battle.

The October section itself does not call him a novik. From the list of 1627/28 it is known that he was a novik of the 1621/22 enrolment with an oklad of 100 chetverti. The different forms Onfilogov and Anfilogov within one file refer to the same Mark Karpovich.
