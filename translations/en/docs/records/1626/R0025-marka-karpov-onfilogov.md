# R0025 — Marka Karpov syn Onfilogov, 2 July 1626

## Original spelling in the publication

> Марка Карпов сын Онфилогов.

> *Translation:* Marka, son of Karp, Onfilogov.

## Extracted facts

- normalized name: Mark Karpovich;
- service town: Oryol;
- surname form: **Онфилогов** (Onfilogov);
- appeared in person at the muster of the second half of the servicemen on 2 July 1626;
- the place of the muster and of service was Mtsensk.

## Who reviewed and where the men were assembled

The reviewing officers were the stolnik and voivode **Ivan Ivanovich Buturlin** and the voivode **Leonty Agafonovich Izvolsky**. The second half took over from 1 July in place of the first, which had been released, and remained with them in the regiments at Mtsensk.

The assembly was connected with guarding the southern frontier amid reports of Tatar raids in Elets, Novosil and Voronezh districts. Readiness for battle, scouting and defence of the towns against ruin and captivity were required. No more distant campaign point is given for Mark.

## Novik

In the line of 1626 Mark is not called a novik. His status is reconstructed from the list of 1627/28: there he stands among the noviki of the 1621/22 enrolment with a pomestye oklad of 100 chetverti. So he was a novik enrolled earlier, already serving in the second half of the Mtsensk garrison assembly.
