# R0029 — Vaska Dmitreyev syn Onpilogov, 17 April 1626

## Original spelling in the publication

> Васка Дмитреев сын Онпилогов.

> *Translation:* Vaska, son of Dmitry, Onpilogov.

## Extracted facts

- normalized name: Vasily Dmitriyevich;
- surname form: **Онпилогов** (Onpilogov);
- service town: Oryol;
- on 17 April 1626 he was listed among those present at the muster in Mtsensk.

## Who conducted the muster

The list was compiled «по смотру» ("from the muster") of the stolnik and voivode **Ivan Ivanovich Buturlin** and the voivode **Leonty Agafonovich Izvolsky**. Vasily is in the Oryol section of the general list of the dvoryane and deti boyarskie of Belyov, Bolkhov, Karachev, Mtsensk, Oryol, Novosil and Chern.

This confirms personal attendance, not merely the entry of a name in absentia: the section opens with the formula «ести» ("present"), set against a separate list of «нетей» (neti) — those who failed to appear.

## Why the servicemen were assembled

The voivodes assembled the servicemen of several southern towns for regimental and frontier service. A subsequent report mentions sending part of the first half to Valuyki «для посольской размены» ("for the ambassadorial exchange"), but there is no such assignment against Vasily's name. Only the muster and his presence in Mtsensk are reliably established.

## Was he a novik

Vasily is not called a novik. His inclusion in the general Oryol list without a formula of first enrolment, and then in the gorodovye (town servicemen) entry of 1627/28, shows a man already in service. The exact year of his initial enrolment is unknown.

## Continued service

On 7 June of the same year Vasily appears again in the list of the first half, and in 1627/28 he receives a separate oklad entry. These are three independent documentary events, not duplicates of one line.

## Source and limitations

The line is known from a complete published transcription. An image of fol. 35 has not yet been obtained, so the surname form and literal reading are still to be checked against the manuscript.

## What it does not prove

- that Vasily himself was sent to Valuyki;
- participation in battle;
- any specific horse and weapons in April 1626;
- his exact rank within the corporation or the size of his pomestye.
