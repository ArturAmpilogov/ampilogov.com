# R0030 — Vaska Dmitreyev syn Onpilogov, 7 June 1626

## Original spelling in the publication

> Васка Дмитреев сын Онпилогов.

> *Translation:* Vaska, son of Dmitry, Onpilogov.

## Extracted facts

- Vasily Dmitriyevich was reviewed again in Mtsensk on 7 June 1626;
- the surname is again recorded as **Онпилогов** (Onpilogov);
- he was in the Oryol part of the **first half** of the servicemen;
- the name is in the list of those who appeared in person, not in the subsequent list of netchiki (absentees).

## A second muster

On 6 June the voivodes received a tsar's charter countersigned by the clerk Mikhail Danilov, ordering them to re-review at once all the servicemen present and to report who had appeared and who was absent. On 7 June **Ivan Ivanovich Buturlin** and **Leonty Agafonovich Izvolsky** held a new muster of the first half.

For Vasily this is stronger evidence than a simple repetition of the April list: he was again recognised as present almost two months after the first muster.

## Where service took place

The charter required the absent to be gathered and ordered to be on the sovereign's service with the voivodes **at Mtsensk**. So Mtsensk was not only the place of the roll call but also the appointed station of regimental service for the first half.

The document also speaks of assigning some servicemen to Valuyki for the ambassadorial exchange. However, Vasily's name carries no individual note of such a dispatch, so his confirmed place of service remains Mtsensk.

## Status

Vasily is not called a novik here. Nor is there information on a horse, a pishchal (matchlock gun) or an oklad: the June section records attendance, not full combat equipment. The pomestye oklad of 200 chetverti appears only in the list of 1627/28.

## Source and limitations

The entry is based on a published transcription of the whole file. Manuscript folio 65 is not yet available; the date of 7 June is established from the voivodes' report and the heading of the first-half section containing the line.

## What it does not prove

- that Vasily served at Valuyki itself;
- participation in a combat engagement;
- continuous presence between 17 April and 7 June;
- his family or kinship ties.
