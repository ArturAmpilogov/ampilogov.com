# R0023 — Ivashka Vasilyev syn Onfilogov, 2 July 1626

## Original spelling in the publication

> Ивашка Васильев сын Онфилогов.

> *Translation:* Ivashka, son of Vasily, Onfilogov.

## Extracted facts

- Ivan belonged to the Oryol service corporation;
- the surname is recorded in the form **Онфилогов** (Onfilogov);
- on 2 July 1626 he appeared in person at the muster in Mtsensk;
- he belonged to the **second half** of the servicemen, who took over service from 1 July.

## Who conducted the muster

The muster was conducted by the stolnik and voivode **Ivan Ivanovich Buturlin** and the voivode **Leonty Agafonovich Izvolsky**. The general Mtsensk roll was begun on 17 April, but this line is in a separate July section: it is not the April roll call but the appearance of the second half on 2 July.

## Why the army was assembled

The first half of the servicemen was released from Mtsensk to go home from 1 July, and the second half was to replace it «на государеве службе» ("in the sovereign's service") with the voivodes in the regiments. The voivodes reported news of Tatar attacks on Elets, Novosil and Voronezh districts. The servicemen were ordered to be ready, to send out scouts and to defend the southern towns against raids and the capture of their inhabitants.

Mtsensk was the place of the muster and of Ivan's further stay on service. His line contains no specific campaign destination or order to march out of Mtsensk.

## Was he a novik

In this section Ivan is not called a novik (newly enrolled serviceman). The list of 1627/28 places him among the gorodovye (town servicemen) with an oklad of 150 chetverti, not among the noviki. So this is a man already performing ordinary town service.
