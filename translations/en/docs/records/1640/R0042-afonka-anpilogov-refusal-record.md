# R0042 — The Oryol Man Afonka Anpilogov and the Form «Аеонька Фнеилогов», 1640

## Quotation from the publication

> В отказе 1640 г. орленин Афонька Анпилогов означил себя так: Аеонька Фнеилогов (253).

> *Translation:* In a refusal record of 1640 the Oryol man Afonka Anpilogov designated himself thus: Aeonka Fneilogov (253).

## What happened

Afonka Anpilogov took part in drawing up a refusal record (otkaz) of 1640. Refusal records were the acts by which, after rights and boundaries had been checked, a holder was put in possession of land. In T. F. Vashchenko's article the ordinary rendering of his name — `Афонька Анпилогов` (Afonka Anpilogov) — is compared with the unusual form in which he "designated himself": `Аеонька Фнеилогов` (Aeonka Fneilogov).

This is a rare record not only of the surname but of an individual pronunciation or spelling. However, what is available is a linguist's quotation, not an image of the refusal book itself. So it cannot be decided whether Afonka wrote these words in his own hand, dictated them to a scribe, or whether the form arose when the act was copied.

## The form of the name and surname

- `Афонька` (Afonka) is a diminutive documentary form of the name Afanasy;
- `Аеонька` (Aeonka) reflects a graphic or phonetic substitution in the name;
- `Фнеилогов` (Fneilogov) is especially important to keep literally: the form differs sharply from the usual `Анпилогов` and shows how unstable the rendering of the surname could be in live speech and cursive script;
- this is no reason to normalize the whole family as Fneilogovs: alongside it the publication itself calls the man Afonka Anpilogov.

## Status and historical context

In the source he is called an **орленин** (orlenin), that is, a man of Oryol. His very participation in a refusal record places him in a context of land law, but the short quotation gives neither the size of his holding nor his exact service rank. So the earlier description "service landholder" should be treated as probable context, not a literally established status.

In 1640 Oryol was a southern frontier fortress of the Russian state. Land refusal records here were closely bound up with the settlement of the service population, but this fragment is not a military muster.

## Was this a muster, and where was the army assembled

No. This is not a desyatnya, not a muster roll, not a review of noviki and not a campaign list. No one conducting a military muster is named in the quotation. A place of assembly or a campaign direction is absent. Afonka cannot be called a novik on the basis of this entry.

## What it proves

- the existence of the Oryol man Afonka Anpilogov in a refusal record of 1640;
- the coexistence of the forms `Анпилогов` and `Фнеилогов` for one person;
- the value of this form as evidence of the unstable rendering of name and surname.

## What it does not prove

- that `Аеонька Фнеилогов` is a signature in his own hand;
- Afonka's exact rank, pomestye oklad or the composition of his holding;
- participation in a military muster or campaign;
- kinship with other Oryol Anpilogovs.

## What to look for next

The full archival reference of the 1640 refusal record and an image of its original folio are needed. Only the original will make it possible to check the reading, the scribe's hand and whether Afonka possibly signed it himself.
