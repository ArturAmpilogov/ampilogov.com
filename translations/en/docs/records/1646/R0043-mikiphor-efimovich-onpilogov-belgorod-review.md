# R0043 — Mikifor Yefimovich Onpilogov at the Belgorod review, 20 July 1646

## Original spelling

> По 5 рублев. […] Микифор Ефимов сын Онпилогов.

> *Translation:* At 5 roubles each. […] Mikifor, son of Yefim, Onpilogov.

## Extracted facts

- full name: **Mikifor Yefimov syn Onpilogov** ("Mikifor, son of Yefim, Onpilogov");
- the surname is written with an initial `Он-`: `Онпилогов`;
- he was in the group with a money oklad (salary entitlement) of **5 roubles**;
- the entry belongs to the Belgorod town servicemen;
- there is no individual description of horse, weapons, armour or state of health next to his name.

## Who held the review

The review was held by the Belgorod stolnik and voevoda (governor) **Prince Fyodor Andreyevich Khilkov**. He acted under a tsar's decree and a charter from the Razryad Prikaz (Military Service Chancellery) signed by the dyak (secretary) **Grigory Larionov**. The servicemen were ordered to be gathered in the Belgorod syezzhaya, or prikaznaya, izba (the town's administrative office) and inspected in person.

Elected okladchiki (assessors of entitlements) took part in assigning the oklads: **Ivan Maslov, Nefyod Prokhorov, Larion Orekhov, Klim Mezentsov, Ivan Logvinov, Merkula Panteleyev and Firs Filatov**. The cash — 2,040 roubles — and the service list were brought by **Zosima Maslov**.

## Why the review was held

The aim was not to send a regiment on a particular campaign but to put local service in order:

- to summon the Belgorod dvoryane and deti boyarskie (provincial gentry servicemen);
- to inspect them face to face;
- to announce and pay out the money salary;
- to check the servicemen against the lists;
- to record those who were serving but had not yet been formally enrolled;
- to note separately the old, the maimed and those who had not received money.

That is why the five-rouble amount matters as an indication of Mikifor's place within the Belgorod service corporation, but not as pay for any one campaign.

## Was Mikifor a novik

Later in the published desyatnya (service register) there is a separate section of noviki (newly enrolled servicemen) enrolled earlier, in the year 143/144. Mikifor stands not there but in the main list of town servicemen. Therefore **in this entry he is not a novik**. This does not prove that he had never before been enrolled as a novik.

## Where the troops were to assemble

The document orders attendance at the Belgorod administrative office for an in-person review and payment of salary. No specific place of a later campaign is named for Mikifor's group. So he cannot be credited with an assembly on the Don, the Crimean campaign or any other army. The line below about Isay Dumin, who «из Белагорода сшол на Дон» ("left Belgorod for the Don"), concerns a different man.

## Historical context

In the mid-17th century Belgorod was a key fortress of the southern frontier. In the 1640s the construction of the Belgorod Line was being completed, and the state was refining the make-up of garrisons and the land and money oklads of servicemen. The desyatnya records the administrative side of this system: who was obliged to serve, who appeared and what oklad was due to him.

## What it proves

- Mikifor's given name, patronymic and surname form;
- his inclusion in the Belgorod service list;
- a money oklad of five roubles;
- his participation in the administrative review of 20 July 1646.

## What it does not prove

- the size of his land oklad: the nearest headings giving numbers of chetverti cannot be mechanically carried across the structure of the page;
- his weapons and whether he had a horse;
- the direction of a campaign;
- identity with Mikifor Anpilogov of the village of Sazhnoye.

## What to look for next

An image of the original folio of the desyatnya is needed. A separate task is to compare this Mikifor with the Sazhnoye entry by patronymic, holding, neighbours and chronology, without merging the men in advance.
