# R0045 — The household of Filipp Dementyevich Anpilogov in Afanasyevskoye, 1684

## Original spelling

> Село Афонасьевское на реке на Большой Рыбинке. В селе церковь во имя Святого Афонасия и Кирилла. Усадища к реке к Большой Рыбинке, а на усадищах дворы помещиков обоянцев детей боярских: […] Филип Дементьев сын Анпилогов.

> *Translation:* The village of Afonasyevskoye on the river Bolshaya Rybinka. In the village is a church in the name of Saints Afonasy and Kirill. The homestead plots run down to the river Bolshaya Rybinka, and on the homestead plots are the households of landholders, Oboyan deti boyarskie: […] Filip, son of Dementy, Anpilogov.

## Extracted facts

- full name: **Filip Dementyev syn Anpilogov** ("Filip, son of Dementy, Anpilogov");
- status: an Oboyan **syn boyarsky** (provincial gentry serviceman) and landholder;
- his household stood on a homestead plot in the village of Afanasyevskoye by the Bolshaya Rybinka;
- the village had a church of Saints Afanasy and Kirill;
- Filipp is listed among a group of households of local landholders.

## Who compiled the book

The description, measurement and boundary survey were carried out by the scribes:

- the stolnik **Avraam Bogdanovich Manturov**;
- the clerk **Grigory Zherebtsov**.

They acted by decree of the co-rulers Ioann Alekseyevich and Pyotr Alekseyevich and on the instructions of the Razryad Prikaz (Military Service Chancellery) signed by the dyak (secretary) **N. Kobyakov**.

## Why the boundary survey was held

The scribes were ordered to describe, measure and demarcate the lands of Oboyan and its district: church and monastery holdings, the lands of streltsy, Cossacks, gunners and gatekeepers, as well as the pomestye and votchina (service and hereditary) estates of dvoryane, deti boyarskie, widows, unmarried women and nedorosli (sons not yet of service age). Particular attention was paid to lands left incompletely described and demarcated in the previous year.

Filipp's entry stands in the topographical description of homestead households. It reliably shows the location of the household and his social estate, but the fragment quoted does not give the area of his own ploughland, hay meadows or woodland.

## Service status and military context

A syn boyarsky represented local service landholding: the landholder's household and the obligation of the sovereign's service were parts of a single system. However, the book of 1684 is **not a review of troops**. It does not check Filipp's attendance, horse, weapons or fitness for combat.

Filipp is not called a novik (newly enrolled serviceman). Nor is there any indication of where troops were assembling: the scribes worked on the spot, describing and demarcating Oboyan district. So this entry allows us to infer his service estate, but not participation in a particular campaign.

## Historical context

By the 1680s Oboyan had long been part of the system of southern fortresses and the Belgorod Line. After decades of settlement the state needed to fix the boundaries of pomestye estates and distinguish old grants, primernye (surplus) lands and disputed plots. The cadastral book no longer shows the moment of a fortress's founding but a mature network of villages and homesteads of service landholders.

## The local milieu

Filipp is listed next to Luka and Ivan Kononov, Ivan Polyansky and his companions — Fatey Babkin, Fyodor Belousov, Ivan Plotnikov, Artemy Trubcheninov, Trofim Bashkotov, Timofey Sukhanov, Sila Kryukov, Luchka and Kondrashka Boyev. These neighbours are useful for further searches in otkaznye (land-transfer), boundary and service books.

## What it proves

- Filipp's given name, patronymic and surname;
- that he belonged to the Oboyan deti boyarskie;
- that he had a landholder's household in Afanasyevskoye on the Bolshaya Rybinka;
- that the household was included in the cadastral and boundary description of 1684.

## What it does not prove

- the exact area of his holding;
- the members of his household or whether he had peasants;
- military readiness or participation in a campaign;
- that he was a novik;
- kinship with other Anpilogovs.

## What to look for next

The original folio of the cadastral book and the related boundary plans or land-transfer records are needed. The list of neighbours provides a control group for finding Filipp in the Oboyan desyatni (service registers) and land disputes.
