# R0003 — Garasim Ivanov syn Onpilogov, 1655

## Original spelling

> Гарасим Ивановъ с(ы)нъ Онпилогов.

> *Translation:* Garasim, son of Ivan, Onpilogov.

## Full transcription

Гарасим Иванов сын Онпилогов.

*Translation:* Garasim, son of Ivan, Onpilogov.

The abbreviation `с(ы)нъ` is expanded as «сын» ("son"). The given-name form `Гарасим` (Garasim) is kept as in the document; in the modern index it is normalised as Gerasim.

## Extracted facts

- given name in the document: Garasim;
- patronymic in its 17th-century form: Ivanov syn ("son of Ivan");
- surname form: Onpilogov;
- place in the list: the section of the servicemen of Oskol;
- document: the list of the regiment of the boyar V. V. Buturlin, 1655.

## What it proves

- Garasim Ivanov syn Onpilogov is named in the Oskol part of the regimental list of 1655;
- the document directly records the surname form `Онпилогов`.

## What it does not prove

- that he is the father of Mikhail and Karp, named in the list of 1658;
- Garasim's kinship with other Oskol or Oryol bearers of the surname.

## Reading notes

- the line has been checked against the full folio and the enlarged fragment;
- a possible match with the father of the brothers of 1658 requires a separate document: agreement of name and place is not enough.
