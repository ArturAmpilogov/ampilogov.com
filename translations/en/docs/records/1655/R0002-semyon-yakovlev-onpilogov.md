# R0002 — Semyon Yakovlev syn Onpilogov, 1655

## Original spelling

> Семен Яковлев с(ы)нъ Онпилогов.

> *Translation:* Semyon, son of Yakov, Onpilogov.

## Full transcription

Семён Яковлев сын Онпилогов.

*Translation:* Semyon, son of Yakov, Onpilogov.

The abbreviation `с(ы)нъ` is expanded as «сын» ("son"). The given name and surname are rendered without modern normalisation.

## Extracted facts

- given name in the document: Semyon;
- patronymic in its 17th-century form: Yakovlev syn ("son of Yakov");
- surname form: Onpilogov;
- place in the list: the section of the servicemen of Kursk;
- document: the list of the regiment of the boyar V. V. Buturlin, 1655.
