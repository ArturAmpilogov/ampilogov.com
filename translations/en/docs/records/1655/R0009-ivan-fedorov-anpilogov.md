# R0009 — Ivan Fyodorov syn Anpilogov, 1655

## Original spelling

> Иван Федоров сын Анпилогов.

> *Translation:* Ivan, son of Fyodor, Anpilogov.

## Full transcription

Иван Федоров сын Анпилогов.

*Translation:* Ivan, son of Fyodor, Anpilogov.

The given name, patronymic and surname are rendered without modern normalisation.

## Extracted facts

- given name in the document: Ivan;
- patronymic in its 17th-century form: Fyodorov syn ("son of Fyodor");
- surname form: Anpilogov;
- place in the list: the Oryol section, under the rubric «Городовые» ("Town servicemen");
- oklad group: 250 chetverti;
- document: the list of the regiment of the boyar V. V. Buturlin, 1655.

## What it proves

- Ivan Fyodorov syn Anpilogov is named on a separate line in the Oryol part of the regimental list of 1655;
- the scribe placed him in the group of town servicemen with a pomestye oklad (land entitlement) of 250 chetverti;
- the document directly records the surname form `Анпилогов`.

## What it does not prove

- that Ivan actually held or ploughed 250 chetverti of land: this is the size of the oklad, not an inventory of land in use;
- Ivan's kinship with other Oryol bearers of the surname;
- his family and later service.

## Reading notes

- the line has been checked against the full folio and the enlarged fragment;
- the new rubric «С отцовского поместья» ("From the father's pomestye") begins immediately after Ivan's line, so it does not apply to him;
- the modern form "Ivan Fyodorovich" is used only in the index of people.
