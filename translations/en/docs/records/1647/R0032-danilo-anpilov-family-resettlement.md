# R0032 — Danilo Anpilov with two sons, resettlement in 1646/47

## Original entry and published extract

> Данило Левонтьев сын Анпилов […] у него два сына: Кондрашка женат, да
> Кондрашко не женат.

> *Translation:* Danilo, son of Levonty, Anpilov […] he has two sons: Kondrashka, married, and
> Kondrashko, unmarried.

The full published extract reports that Danilo was chosen together with his family
«на вечное житьё» ("for permanent residence") in the New Tsaryov-Alekseyev town in the year 7155.

## The family

- head of the family — **Danilo Leontyevich Anpilov**;
- son **Kondrashka** — married;
- second son **Kondrashko** — unmarried.

The identical form of the sons' names is not a database error: the scribe really
distinguishes the two sons only by the ending of the name and their marital status. The patronymic
Danilovich and the surname Anpilov are restored from the direct formula «у него два
сына» ("he has two sons"); in the short line they are not repeated after each name.

## What «на вечное житьё» meant

This was not a temporary campaign or an ordinary posting. The family was taken from its former
place of service and fixed as permanent residents of a new frontier town.
Danilo's household was to move with him; the service household
was set up anew in Tsaryov-Alekseyev.

The town was being built in 1647 where the Bely Kolodez flows into the Oskol as a new
fortress of the Belgorod Line. It covered a dangerous stretch of the Tatar roads and
was to hold a permanent garrison. So the resettlement of Danilo and his family
was part of populating and organising the fortress militarily, not an independent
colonising journey.

## Who organised the resettlement

The individual line does not name the person who actually chose Danilo.
The construction and initial organisation of Tsaryov-Alekseyev were directed by Prince
**Vasily Petrovich Lvov**, the first voevoda (governor) of the new town. But he cannot be credited
with personally inspecting the family on the strength of this line alone.

## Service status

In the neighbouring construction book Danilo is directly called a regimental Cossack transferred
from Oskol. He was not a novik (newly enrolled serviceman): the document records the transfer of a Cossack already in service
to a new garrison. The ages of Danilo and both sons are not given.

## Image and limitations

[The saved fragment of the line](/archive/evidence/rgada/f210-op6d-d20/l12.jpg)
confirms the names and the family formula. The year 7155 ran from September 1646 to
August 1647; without a month, the choice of the family cannot be dated more precisely.

## What it does not prove

- the names of Danilo's wife and of the elder Kondrashka's wife;
- the sons' ages;
- the family's participation in battle;
- that Prince Lvov personally made the selection.
