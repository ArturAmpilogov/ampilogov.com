# R0040 — Confrontation of Kirill Anpilogov with the archpriest Grigory, 10 or 14 August 1648

## Original spelling

> ...Кузька Воденицын, Богдашка Иконник, Кирюшка Анпилогов с протопопом с очей на очи ставлены...

> *Translation:* …Kuzka Vodenitsyn, Bogdashka Ikonnik and Kiryushka Anpilogov were brought face to face with the archpriest…

## Full transcription

И у пытки Кузька Воденицын, Богдашка Иконник, Кирюшка Анпилогов с протопопом с очей на очи ставлены и на очной ставке сказали: от протопопа де они на голову на Костянтина в убийстве наученья не слыхали.

*Translation:* And under torture Kuzka Vodenitsyn, Bogdashka Ikonnik and Kiryushka Anpilogov were brought face to face with the archpriest, and at the confrontation they said: they had not heard from the archpriest any incitement to kill the commander Konstantin.

The sense continues on the next page of the publication: the accused explained that the order had been passed on to them by the archpriest's son, the priest Ivan, supposedly in his father's name, but whether it was a real order of the archpriest Grigory they did not know.

## Extracted facts

- Kirill took part in a collective confrontation with the archpriest Grigory;
- he and the other accused did not confirm that they had personally heard a direct order from the archpriest to kill Teglev;
- they distinguished the words of the priest Ivan from any instruction of his father's own;
- the surname of the same Kirill is printed here as `Анпилогов`, whereas in the neighbouring confrontation it is `Онпилогов`.

## Who conducted the investigative act

The confrontation was part of the inquiry of the stolnik **Vasily Vasilyevich Buturlin** after the repeated torture of 10 and 14 August. The publication does not say which of the two days it was.

## Significance for the surname

The sequence of the file is especially valuable for onomastics: the full formula `Кирюшка Потапов сын Анпилогов`, and then the forms `Онпилогов` and `Анпилогов`, refer to one accused man within one trial. This is direct evidence of wavering in the initial vowel when the surname was written down, not grounds for creating three different people.

## Service context

Kirill is not a novik (newly enrolled serviceman) and not a serviceman: he is a peasant of the Bogoroditsky Monastery. The confrontation has nothing to do with a review, a regiment, an assembly or a campaign.

## What it proves

- the identity of the surname forms `Анпилогов` and `Онпилогов` for Kirill within the file;
- that Kirill did not claim to have personally heard an order from the archpriest Grigory.

## What it does not prove

- the innocence or guilt of the archpriest;
- the absence of pressure on the participants in the confrontation;
- Kirill's kinship with other Anpilogovs of Kursk.
