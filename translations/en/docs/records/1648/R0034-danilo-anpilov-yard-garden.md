# R0034 — Danilo Anpilov: a new household plot and kitchen garden, 15 May 1648

## Original spelling

> Данило Анпилов.

> *Translation:* Danilo Anpilov.

## Extracted facts

- Danilo is among the 70 transferees from Oskol — regimental Cossacks of the new
  Tsaryov-Alekseyev;
- he was allotted a plot for a household and kitchen garden measuring **25 × 10 sazhens**;
- the area of the rectangular plot was 250 square sazhens;
- the allotment was made on 15 May 1648 beyond the Bely Kolodez, opposite the sloboda (settlement) of the
  Yelets regimental Cossacks.

## Who allotted the plot

The work was carried out by **Voin Antipyevich Annenkov** by the sovereign's decree and on the orders of the
stolnik and voevoda (governor) **Ivan Vasilyevich Olferyev**. Annenkov marked out the plots
in a single row from the foot of the slopes towards the Bely Kolodez, following the planned layout of the
sloboda.

So the direct executor of this land allotment was Annenkov, while
Olferyev represented the voevoda's authority. Neither of them held a military review of Danilo
here.

## Why a second plot appeared

Earlier Danilo had already been allotted a household plot of 20 × 12 sazhens and ploughland. The entry of 15 May concerns
a new layout of the households and kitchen gardens of the Oskol regimental Cossacks beyond the Bely
Kolodez; the construction book states directly that their old homesteads remained theirs. So this is a second property and planning event, not a corrected
size of the first household plot.

The new sloboda kept the regimental Cossacks near the fortress and provided them with
a household during permanent frontier service. The entry contains no campaign direction or
combat episode.

## Service status

Danilo remains a regimental Cossack — a transferee from Oskol. He is not called a novik (newly enrolled serviceman) and
is not being enrolled into service anew; the document regulates the placement of a garrison that had already been
formed.

## What it does not prove

- the actual size of the kitchen garden once it was brought into use;
- Danilo's participation in a particular campaign or battle;
- kinship with bearers of the same surname from other service towns.
