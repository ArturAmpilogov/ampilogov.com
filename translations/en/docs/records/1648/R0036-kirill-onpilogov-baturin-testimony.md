# R0036 — Kirill Onpilogov in the testimony of Ivashka Baturin, 8 August 1648

## Original spelling

> И убили де Костянтина Кирюшка Онпилогов, Кузька Воденицын, Ивашка Ильин, Васька Белевцов...

> *Translation:* And Konstantin, he said, was killed by Kiryushka Onpilogov, Kuzka Vodenitsyn, Ivashka Ilyin, Vaska Belevtsov…

## Full transcription

А у съезжей избы двери выбивал и Костянтина мертвого бил. А после сказал, что Костянтина бил живого палкою трожды. Да с ним же де Костянтина били Савоска Фирсов да Орешка Рубцов и иные их товарыщи — весь мир, а имян их, сказал, не упомнит, что их было много. А убить де им велела игуменья и говорила всем мирским людем: «Не убиви де Костентина не ходите». А воевода де высылал протопопа уговаривать, и протопопов де сын, поп Иван, выходя, им говорил, чтоб убили Костянтина Теглева. А приказывал де ево убить отец ево протопоп Григорей. И убили де Костянтина Кирюшка Онпилогов, Кузька Воденицын, Ивашка Ильин, Васька Белевцов Божедомские слободы, Ивашка и Васька не сысканы.

*Translation:* And at the administrative office he broke down the doors and beat Konstantin when he was dead. And afterwards he said that he had beaten Konstantin, still alive, three times with a stick. And with him, he said, Konstantin was beaten by Savoska Firsov and Oreshka Rubtsov and others of their comrades — the whole community, and their names, he said, he cannot remember, as there were many of them. And it was the abbess, he said, who told them to kill him, and she said to all the townspeople: "Do not come back without killing Konstantin." And the voevoda, he said, sent the archpriest out to persuade them, and the archpriest's son, the priest Ivan, came out and told them to kill Konstantin Teglev. And it was his father, the archpriest Grigory, who ordered him killed. And Konstantin, he said, was killed by Kiryushka Onpilogov, Kuzka Vodenitsyn, Ivashka Ilyin and Vaska Belevtsov of the Bozhedomskaya sloboda; Ivashka and Vaska have not been found.

## Extracted facts

- Ivashka Baturin named Kirill Onpilogov among the direct participants in the killing of Konstantin Teglev;
- Baturin linked the incitement to the abbess Feodora, the priest Ivan and the archpriest Grigory;
- two of the men he named — Ivashka Ilyin and Vaska Belevtsov — had not been found by the time of the inquiry;
- the testimony was obtained under torture and therefore requires critical assessment.

## Investigation and context

The interrogation was part of the inquiry of the stolnik **Vasily Vasilyevich Buturlin**, begun after the Kursk uprising of 5 July. On 8 August Buturlin questioned the prisoners separately, using torture. This matters: the recurrence of names in several testimonies strengthens Kirill's documentary link with the case, but does not remove the coerced nature of the confessions.

The local voevoda (governor) **Fyodor Lodyzhensky** tried to contain the situation at the administrative office. The documents also contain mutual accusations by the voevoda, the clergy, the monastery peasants and the servicemen, so the investigation file cannot be read as a neutral chronicle.

## Service position

Kirill is called neither a novik (newly enrolled serviceman), nor a strelets, nor a syn boyarsky. His status, established in the same file, is a peasant of the Bogoroditsky Monastery. The entry has nothing to do with a military review and says nothing about a campaign or an assembly point.

## What it proves

- a second, separate accusatory mention of Kirill Onpilogov in the inquiry materials;
- that Kirill's name appears in one sequence with the other accused in the case.

## What it does not prove

- that Baturin's testimony was independent and voluntary;
- Kirill's exact individual role in dealing the fatal blows;
- any military rank for Kirill.
