# R0041 — The sentence on Kirill Onpilogov, 17 August 1648

## Original spelling

> ...Кирюшку Онпилогова... пяти человек велел вершить и перевешать по дорогам.

> *Translation:* …Kiryushka Onpilogov… these five men he ordered to be executed and hanged along the roads.

## Full transcription

И 156 году августа в 17 д. по государеву [...] указу, стольник Василей Васильевич Бутурлин, выбрав пущих воров и заводчиков, которые в Курске воровской завод завели и к приказной избе приступали и двери выбивали и вытаща из съезжей избы голову Костентина Теглева убили до смерти и животы ево розграбили. А в роспросе и с пытки они в том винились и сами на себя говорили, Богородицкого монастыря крестьянин Куземку Воденицына, Кирюшку Онпилогова, Ивашка Ботурина Малика да Девича монастыря крестьянина Костьку Филшина да новописнова стрелца Богдашка Иконник пяти человек велел вершить и перевешать по дорогам. А достольных товарыщей их воров же и заводчиков, которые к съезжей избе гилем приходили, и приступали, и Костентина Теглева убить просили, и двери выбивали ж и Костентина Теглева били ж и животы ево грабили, а иные мужиков ворочали и на убийство научали, велел бить кнутом по торгом и посадить в тюрьму до государева указу. А игуменья Феодора да протопоп Григорей, да сын его поп Иван и келарь Онтоней и черные попы Хорлампей и Моисей и губной староста Кондратей Беседин отданы за приставов до государева указу.

*Translation:* And in the year 156, on the 17th day of August, by the sovereign's […] decree, the stolnik Vasily Vasilyevich Buturlin, having picked out the worst rebels and ringleaders, who had started the rebellion in Kursk and assailed the administrative office and broken down the doors and, having dragged the commander Konstantin Teglev out of the administrative office, beat him to death and plundered his goods — and under questioning and under torture they confessed to this and incriminated themselves — namely the peasants of the Bogoroditsky Monastery Kuzemka Vodenitsyn, Kiryushka Onpilogov and Ivashka Boturin Malik, and the peasant of the Devichy (Convent) Monastery Kostka Filshin, and the newly enrolled strelets Bogdashka Ikonnik, these five men he ordered to be executed and hanged along the roads. And their remaining comrades, likewise rebels and ringleaders, who came to the administrative office as a mob, and assailed it, and demanded that Konstantin Teglev be killed, and likewise broke down the doors and likewise beat Konstantin Teglev and plundered his goods, and others who turned the men back and incited them to the killing, he ordered to be flogged with the knout in the marketplaces and put in prison until the sovereign's decree. And the abbess Feodora, and the archpriest Grigory, and his son the priest Ivan, and the cellarer Ontoney, and the monk-priests Khorlampey and Moisey, and the gubnoy starosta (district police elder) Kondraty Besedin were handed over to bailiffs until the sovereign's decree.

## Extracted facts

- on 17 August 1648 Kirill Onpilogov was included among the five principal accused;
- the stolnik Vasily Buturlin ordered them to be executed and their bodies hanged along the roads;
- the other active participants were to be flogged with the knout in the marketplace and held in prison until a new decree of the sovereign;
- the representatives of the monastery authorities and the clergy were left with bailiffs, that is, under supervision, until a decision from Moscow.

## Who passed and carried out the sentence

The decision was announced by the stolnik who led the inquiry, **Vasily Vasilyevich Buturlin**, acting «по государеву указу» ("by the sovereign's decree"). The formula `велел вершить` ("ordered to be executed") means an order to carry out the death sentence. The published text is precisely an order for execution; there is no separate report by the executioner or record of the actual execution in this card.

Hanging the bodies along the roads had a public, deterrent purpose: the punishment was meant to be seen by travellers and the local population as a warning against a new uprising.

## Historical context

The Kursk uprising was part of the wave of urban uprisings of 1648, but it had a local cause — the struggle over the monastery slobody (settlements) and the actions of the streltsy commander Konstantin Teglev. The sentence came only six weeks after the events of 5 July and concluded a rapid inquiry with torture and confrontations.

## Kirill's service position

Kirill is called a peasant of the Bogoroditsky Monastery. He was not a novik (newly enrolled serviceman), did not undergo a review and was not sent on a campaign. The death sentence was passed for taking part in an urban uprising and the killing of an official, not for a breach of military service.

## What it proves

- that a death sentence was passed on Kirill Onpilogov on 17 August 1648;
- that he was included among the five principal accused;
- the direct link between this sentence and Buturlin's investigation.

## What it does not prove

- by a separate document, the actual time and place of the execution;
- Kirill's kinship with other bearers of the surname;
- that all the confessions on which the sentence was based were given voluntarily.

## Notes on the source

In the published line part of the sovereign's decree is marked by an editorial omission. The original scroll of the Belgorod Desk, No. 269, has not yet been examined.
