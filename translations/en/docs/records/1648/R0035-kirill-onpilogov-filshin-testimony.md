# R0035 — Kirill Onpilogov in the testimony of Kostka Filshin, 8 August 1648

## Original spelling

> ...двери выбивал с Макарком да с Богдашком Иконником, с Ывашком Батуриным да с Кирюшкою Онпилоговым.

> *Translation:* …he broke down the doors with Makarko and with Bogdashko Ikonnik, with Yvashko Baturin and with Kiryushka Onpilogov.

## Full transcription

Того ж монастыря крестьянин Костька Филшин пытан, а с пытки винился и сказал, что он у приказной избы кричал и Костянтина убить прошал, и двери выбивал с Макарком да с Богдашком Иконником, с Ывашком Батуриным да с Кирюшкою Онпилоговым. И вытаща голову Костентина убили до смерти. А Кузька де им говорил, как они шли к съезжей избе: убьемте Костентина Теглева. А он де Костянтинову убойству будет починщик. И у Костянтина на дворе он был и вино пил и на лошади ево ездил. А что де он в роспросе сказал на воеводу на Федора Лодыженсково, что он посылал россылщика Филку Шилова мужиков ворочать, и то де он Костька на воеводу затеял, потому что воевода их в том убийстве имает. И к протопопу де на двор приходил и говорил: «Хорони де изменника Костянтина Теглева. Атаманы де молодцы миром велели ево схоронить».

*Translation:* Kostka Filshin, a peasant of the same monastery, was tortured, and under torture he confessed and said that he had shouted at the prikaznaya izba (administrative office) and demanded that Konstantin be killed, and had broken down the doors with Makarko and with Bogdashko Ikonnik, with Yvashko Baturin and with Kiryushka Onpilogov. And having dragged out the golova (commander) Konstantin, they beat him to death. And Kuzka, he said, told them as they went to the syezzhaya izba (administrative office): let us kill Konstantin Teglev. And he, Kuzka, would be the instigator of Konstantin's killing. And he had been in Konstantin's yard, drunk wine and ridden his horse. And as for what he had said under questioning against the voevoda Fyodor Lodyzhensky — that he had sent the messenger Filka Shilov to turn the men back — that, he said, he, Kostka, had made up against the voevoda, because the voevoda was holding them for that killing. And he had come to the archpriest's yard and said: "Bury the traitor Konstantin Teglev. The atamans, the brave lads, by common accord have ordered him to be buried."

## Extracted facts

- Kostka Filshin named Kirill Onpilogov as one of the men with whom he broke down the doors of the Kursk administrative office;
- according to his testimony, after the doors were broken down, the streltsy commander Konstantin Teglev was dragged out of the building and killed;
- the testimony was obtained under torture and is not independent voluntary evidence;
- Kirill's surname is given in the form `Онпилогов`.

## Who conducted the investigation

The investigation was conducted by the stolnik **Vasily Vasilyevich Buturlin**. At the start of the relevant section of the file it says that on 8 August he questioned the arrested men separately «у пытки» ("under torture"). Fyodor Lodyzhensky was the Kursk voevoda (governor) during the uprising, but Filshin's testimony was recorded by Buturlin's inquiry.

## Historical context

The uprising took place in Kursk on 5 July 1648. The immediate conflict was linked to the actions of the streltsy commander Konstantin Teglev against the monastery slobody (settlements) and to the struggle between the town and the monasteries over people, their tax obligations and service status. A charter received from Moscow limited Teglev's further actions; its reading in the administrative office grew into a clash.

The participants' speeches repeated the Moscow example — «на Москве и не таких побивают» ("in Moscow they kill bigger men than him"), that is, news of the Moscow uprising of 1648 was used to justify the violence. This does not mean a formally organised link with Moscow.

## Kirill's service position

This is not a review of troops, not a razbor (service review) and not a campaign list. Kirill is not called a novik (newly enrolled serviceman) or a fighting man: in his own interrogation he is identified as a peasant of the Kursk Bogoroditsky (Mother of God) Monastery. The document contains no assembly point or campaign assignment.

## What it proves

- that Filshin named Kirill Onpilogov among the participants in the attack;
- that the surname form `Онпилогов` is present in the published text of the investigation file.

## What it does not prove

- that all the details of the testimony are true: it was obtained under torture;
- Kirill's kinship with other Anpilogovs;
- that Kirill belonged to the service class.
