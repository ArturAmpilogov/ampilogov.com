# R0038 — Kirill Onpilogov in the testimony of Bogdashka Ikonnik, 8 August 1648

## Original spelling

> ...с Кирюшкою Онпилоговым да с Костькою Филшиным, да с Ывашком Батуриным с Малейком...

> *Translation:* …with Kiryushka Onpilogov and with Kostka Filshin, and with Yvashko Baturin Maleyko…

## Full transcription

Новописной стрелец Богдашка Иконник пытан и с пытки винился: у приказной избы кричал и Костянтина убить прошал, и двери выбивал, и с Кирюшкою Онпилоговым да с Костькою Филшиным, да с Ывашком Батуриным с Малейком, с Федькою Чюрюпиным, с Макарком стрелцом, с Федькою Слоновым и с мирскими людми Костянтина убили до смерти. А животов де Костянтиновых взял он Богдашко сковородку белою да косу да лоскут однорядочной. Да с ним же де грабил Савоска Фирсов и все мирские люди. А велел де им убить Костянтина Кузька Воденицын да протопопов сын, поп Иван им говорил же: убейте де Костянтина Теглева. А велел де убить отец ево протопоп Григорей. А на Куземку Воденицына в воровском заводе говорил прежние свои речи.

*Translation:* The newly enrolled strelets Bogdashka Ikonnik was tortured, and under torture he confessed: he had shouted at the administrative office and demanded that Konstantin be killed, and had broken down the doors, and with Kiryushka Onpilogov and with Kostka Filshin, and with Yvashko Baturin Maleyko, with Fedka Chyuryupin, with Makarko the strelets, with Fedka Slonov and with the townspeople they beat Konstantin to death. And of Konstantin's goods, he said, he, Bogdashko, took a tinned frying pan, a scythe and a piece of odnoryadka cloth. And with him, he said, Savoska Firsov and all the townspeople plundered. And it was Kuzka Vodenitsyn, he said, who told them to kill Konstantin, and the archpriest's son, the priest Ivan, also told them: kill Konstantin Teglev. And it was his father, the archpriest Grigory, who ordered the killing. And against Kuzemka Vodenitsyn as the instigator of the rebellion he repeated his earlier statements.

## Extracted facts

- the newly enrolled strelets Bogdashka Ikonnik named Kirill Onpilogov among the participants in breaking down the doors and killing Teglev;
- next to Kirill the list names monastery peasants, streltsy and other townspeople;
- Ikonnik admitted taking Teglev's property;
- the testimony was obtained under torture.

## Investigation and service context

The inquiry was conducted by the stolnik **Vasily Vasilyevich Buturlin**. Unlike Ikonnik, who is designated a `новописной стрелец` (newly enrolled strelets), Kirill nowhere receives a streltsy or any other military rank: he is a monastery peasant. The word `новописной` refers to Bogdashka, not to Kirill.

The document is not a muster list. There is no inspection of horse and weapons, no enrolment and no assignment to a campaign. The mention of different service groups reflects the mixed make-up of the urban crowd, not the formation of a regiment.

## What it proves

- one more mention of Kirill Onpilogov, independent within the structure of the file;
- the difference in status between Kirill and the newly enrolled strelets Bogdashka Ikonnik.

## What it does not prove

- that the testimony was voluntary;
- that Kirill was a strelets or underwent a military review;
- the exact sequence of blows and individual responsibility for the death.
