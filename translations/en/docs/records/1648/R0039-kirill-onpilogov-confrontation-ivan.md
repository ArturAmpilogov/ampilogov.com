# R0039 — Confrontation of Kirill Onpilogov with the priest Ivan, 10 or 14 August 1648

## Original spelling

> А Кирюшка Онпилогов на очной ставке с попом Иваном говорил...

> *Translation:* And Kiryushka Onpilogov, at the confrontation with the priest Ivan, said…

## Full transcription

А Кирюшка Онпилогов на очной ставке с попом Иваном говорил: тот де поп Иван приходил к ним, приходил и шапкою махал и убить Костянтина велел по отцову велению.

*Translation:* And Kiryushka Onpilogov, at the confrontation with the priest Ivan, said: that priest Ivan came to them, came and waved his cap and told them to kill Konstantin at his father's bidding.

## Extracted facts

- Kirill was brought face to face with the priest Ivan;
- Kirill repeated the accusation: Ivan had supposedly waved his cap as a signal and passed on his father's order to kill Teglev;
- the priest Ivan denied the original version, but after repeated torture admitted that he had himself spoken of an order passed on from his father, while maintaining that he had invented it without his father's knowledge;
- this section of the file dates the repeated torture to 10 and 14 August, but does not allow this particular confrontation to be assigned to either of the two days.

## Who conducted the confrontation

The confrontation was part of the inquiry of the stolnik **Vasily Vasilyevich Buturlin**. Before it, Kirill was again «пытали накрепко и огнём жгли» ("tortured hard and burned with fire"). So the repetition of his earlier words cannot be considered a free confirmation, independent of pressure from the investigators.

## Historical and service context

The main question of this part of the inquiry was whether the order to kill came from the Kursk clergy and monastery authorities or whether the accused were shifting the blame. This is a judicial and investigative act, not a military review. Kirill still appears as a monastery peasant, not a novik (newly enrolled serviceman); no troops were assembled and no campaign was assigned.

## What it proves

- that a confrontation between Kirill and the priest Ivan took place;
- that this entry keeps the surname form `Онпилогов`;
- the content of the accusation repeated by Kirill.

## What it does not prove

- that the priest Ivan really acted on his father's orders;
- that Kirill's words after repeated torture were voluntary;
- the exact day of the confrontation within the pair of dates 10/14 August.

## Notes on dating

The document's formula «августа в 10 день да в 14 день колодники пытаны» ("on the 10th day of August and on the 14th day the prisoners were tortured") covers the following block. The publication does not single out a more precise date for this particular confrontation.
