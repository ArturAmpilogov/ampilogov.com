# R0001 — Pavel Terentyev syn Anfilogov, 1596

## Transcription

> Павел Терентьев сын Анфилогов.

> *Translation:* Pavel, son of Terenty, Anfilogov.

The reading has been checked against the original and the published transcription of the desyatnya (service register). The spelling of the publication is kept; the expansion of superscript marks is not reconstructed separately.

## Extracted facts

- name in the document: Pavel;
- patronymic in its 17th-century form: Terentyev syn ("son of Terenty");
- surname form: Anfilogov;
- service category: novik (newly enrolled serviceman);
- pomestye oklad (land entitlement): 250 chetverti.
