# R0004 — Mishka and Karpik, Goraskovy deti Onpilogova, 1658

## Original spelling

> Дано Мишка да Карпикъ Горасковы дети Онпилогова.

> *Translation:* Given: Mishka and Karpik, children of Gorasko Onpilogov.

## Full transcription

Дано Мишка да Карпик, Горасковы дети Онпилогова.

*Translation:* Given: Mishka and Karpik, children of Gorasko Onpilogov.

Two people are named in one line. The form `Горасковы дети` ("Gorasko's children") means that Mishka and Karpik are named as sons of Gorasko; `Онпилогова` refers to their father and gives the family's surname form.

## Extracted facts

- Mishka and Karpik are named in one line;
- both are directly called children of Gorasko;
- the father's surname form: Onpilogov;
- place of the document: Oskol;
- document: the list of servicemen of 1658.

## What it proves

- one line of the document names two sons of one father;
- in the Oskol list of 1658 the family is recorded in the form `Онпилогов`.

## What it does not prove

- that their father is Garasim Ivanov syn Onpilogov from the list of 1655;
- the brothers' kinship with other Oskol, Kursk or Oryol lines;
- the exact modern form of the names without editorial normalisation.
