# R0014 — Sergey Grigoryevich Anpilogov, 1651

## Original spelling in the scholarly publication

> Сергей Григорьев сын Анпилогов, на мерине, с пищалью. Детей: сын
> Сидор, 20 лет; Еремка, 10 лет. Государева жалованья дано пять рублев.
> Порука в денгах: Мелентей Овдокимов, Василей Горяинов, Дмитрей Басов.

> *Translation:* Sergey, son of Grigory, Anpilogov, on a gelding, with a pishchal (harquebus). Children: a son
> Sidor, 20 years old; Yeremka, 10 years old. Of the sovereign's salary five roubles were given.
> Sureties for the money: Melenty Ovdokimov, Vasily Goryainov, Dmitry Basov.

## Full transcription

Сергей Григорьев сын Анпилогов, на мерине, с пищалью. Детей: сын Сидор,
20 лет; Еремка, 10 лет. Государева жалованья дано пять рублев. Порука в
денгах: Мелентей Овдокимов, Василей Горяинов, Дмитрей Басов.

*Translation:* Sergey, son of Grigory, Anpilogov, on a gelding, with a pishchal. Children: a son Sidor,
20 years old; Yeremka, 10 years old. Of the sovereign's salary five roubles were given. Sureties for the
money: Melenty Ovdokimov, Vasily Goryainov, Dmitry Basov.

The text is given according to the full scholarly publication of the desyatnya (service register). The editors kept
the source forms of the names and marked the folio break: the entry begins on
fol. 39v, and the following entry is already on fol. 40.

## Extracted facts

- Sergey Grigoryev syn Anpilogov appeared on a gelding and with a pishchal;
- his son Sidor is recorded as twenty years old;
- his son Yeremka is recorded as ten years old;
- Sergey received five roubles of the sovereign's salary;
- Melenty Ovdokimov, Vasily Goryainov and Dmitry Basov stood surety for the money.

## What it proves

- the full family and service entry of Sergey Grigoryevich Anpilogov in Oboyan;
- the names and ages of his two sons;
- the surname form `Анпилогов` in the published text of the file;
- the horse, weapon, amount of the payment and names of the sureties.

## What it does not prove

- identity with Sergey Grigoryevich Onpilogov from the Kursk desyatnya of 1636;
- kinship with other Anpilogovs of Oboyan without a separate documentary link;
- the letter-level features of the original handwriting, until the manuscript folio is obtained.
