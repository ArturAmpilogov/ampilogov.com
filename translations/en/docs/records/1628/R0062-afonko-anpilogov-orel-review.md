# R0062 — Ofonko Osipov syn Onfilogov, Oryol Novik of 1628/29

## Literal reading

> Офонко Осипов сын Онфилогов [индивидуальная поместная характеристика повреждена и продолжается на л. 109 об.].

> *Translation:* Ofonko, son of Osip, Onfilogov [the individual pomestye description is damaged and continues on fol. 109v].

The full name formula is read on the original fol. 109 and independently confirmed by a line-by-line extract from the same file. The entry continues in four lines at the top of fol. 109v, but is crossed by a fold and a later straight line. The section headings make it possible to establish with confidence his first enrolment, grade and oklad; only the individual formula about his actual pomestye is not yet reconstructed.

## Names and meaning

`Афонко` (Afonko) is the documentary colloquial form of the name Afanasy. The ending `-ко` in service books does not denote a child: scribes used such forms for adult men. In 1640 the Oryol writer of a refusal book (otkaznaya kniga) is called `Афонькой Анпилоговым` (Afonka Anpilogov); the forms Afonko and Afonka belong to the same set of name forms.

`Онфилогов` (Onfilogov) is the reading of the full line on the primary folio. The old manuscript alphabetical index of the Razryad archive gave the same form `Онфилоговъ` for fol. 109, while the new open index gives the normalized `Анпилогов Афонька`. Both indexes led to the same entry.

`Осипов сын` ("son of Osip") is a direct patronymic. It was omitted in the first draft transcription of the project because of a line crossing the text. The exact formula connects Ofonko with Ofonasey Osipov syn Anfilogov of 1641/42.

## What happened

Ofonko was placed in the Oryol section of noviki not previously enrolled. In 7137 he was enrolled in the third grade with a pomestye oklad of 100 chetverti and a cash salary of 5 roubles. The damaged individual formula on whether the actual pomestye was held by him or by his father has not yet been fully read.

## Connection with the 1640 entry

The full match of name, patronymic, rare surname and Oryol makes the Ofonko of 1628/29 the same man who in 1641/42 is recorded as Ofonasey Osipov syn Anfilogov. The Oryol man Afonka Anpilogov, who wrote the refusal book of 5 October 1640, is almost certainly an intermediate mention of the same biography, although that publication does not repeat the patronymic.

## Significance for the family's origin

The find supplies the needed intermediate generation and for the first time names his father — Osip. The most likely candidate among the early Oryol holders is Osip Vasilyevich Anfilogov of Bazdyrevo, 1594/95. Town, rare surname, father's name and generation all match, but there is as yet no direct formula identifying the father with this particular Osip Vasilyevich.

## What it proves

- the existence of the Oryol man Ofonko Osipov syn Onfilogov in 1628/29;
- the primary spelling of the surname `Онфилогов` on fol. 109;
- that this entry corresponds to the index forms `Анпилогов Афонька` and `Онфилоговъ, л. 109`;
- his first enrolment in the third grade with an oklad of 100 chetverti and 5 roubles;
- identity with Ofonasey Osipovich Anfilogov of 1641/42.

## What it does not prove

- the exact size of the pomestye actually held by him or his father;
- that the father named Osip is specifically Osip Vasilyevich of Bazdyrevo;
- the family's earlier town or the route of its move to Oryol.
