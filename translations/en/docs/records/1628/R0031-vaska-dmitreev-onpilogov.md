# R0031 — Vaska Dmitreyev syn Onpilogov, Town Serviceman, 1627/28

## Original spelling in the publication

> Городовые. […] По 200 чети. […] Васка Дмитреев сын Онпилогов.

> *Translation:* Town servicemen. […] At 200 chetverti each. […] Vaska, son of Dmitry, Onpilogov.

The ellipses mark other men between the category heading, the oklad-grade heading and Vasily's line.

## Extracted facts

- name: Vaska, that is, Vasily;
- patronymic: Dmitreyev syn ("son of Dmitry");
- surname form: **Онпилогов** (Onpilogov);
- service town: Oryol;
- category: **gorodovoy** (town serviceman);
- pomestye oklad: **200 chetverti**.

## What the category and oklad meant

The gorodovye made up the bulk of the provincial pomestye cavalry and served by their own town. In the internal hierarchy of the list they stood below the vybornye (select) and dvorovye (court) men, but were still full-fledged pomestye servicemen.

200 chetverti is the reckoned norm of pomestye provision. The actual size of settled and ploughed land is established from pomestye and cadastral books.

## Muster, campaign and novik

This is a name list of service grades and oklady, not a register of a new field muster. The name of the actual compiler, a specific regiment and a campaign direction are not given in the published Oryol section.

Vasily stands among the gorodovye, not in the separate section of noviki that begins further down. So the list does not directly count him as a novik of 1627/28 and does not give the year of his initial enrolment.

## Linking the biography

The full name and town match Vaska Dmitreyev syn Onpilogov at the two Mtsensk musters of 1626 and Vasiley Dmitreyev syn Anpilov in the estimate book of 1641/42. The combination of name, patronymic, town and consecutive service makes the identification very likely, although each line is kept as an independent event.
