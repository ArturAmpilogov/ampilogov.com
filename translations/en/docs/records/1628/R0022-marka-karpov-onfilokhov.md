# R0022 — Marka Karpov syn Онфилохов (Onfilokhov), Novik of 1621/22

## Original spelling in the publication

> Новики 130-го году. […] По 100 чети. […] Марка Карпов сын Онфилохов.

> *Translation:* Noviki of the year [7]130. […] At 100 chetverti each. […] Marka, son of Karp, Onfilokhov.

The heading for the noviki is on fol. 128. On the following folios the section continues, with the men distributed by oklad; Mark stands on fol. 133 in the grade of 100 chetverti.

## Extracted facts

- name: Marka, modern form Mark;
- patronymic: Karpov syn ("son of Karp");
- surname in the publication: Онфилохов (Onfilokhov);
- town of registration: Oryol;
- service category: **novik of 7130**, that is, of 1621/22;
- pomestye oklad: **100 chetverti**.

## Why the novik status is not a chance inference from neighbouring lines

The section «Новики 130-го году» ("Noviki of the year [7]130") begins on fol. 128. It is followed by groups of noviki with oklady of 250, 200, 150 and 100 chetverti. No new independent section begins before Mark's line, so the heading applies to him too. This clarification was missing from the old Claude card.

Mark's oklad is half that of Aleksei Vasilyevich in the same section, but this proves neither poverty nor younger age: the oklad was a reckoned service norm and depended on the circumstances of the enrolment.

## Who enrolled him and where the men were assembled

The name of the person who enrolled the noviki in 1621/22 is not given in the available fragment. The list of 1627/28 is not an order to march and does not give a campaign regiment, direction or place of the next assembly.

A year earlier Marka Karpov syn Onfilogov/Anfilogov appears twice in the muster roll at Mtsensk — on fols. 106 and 151. So the place of his muster in 1626 is known, but Mtsensk cannot be transferred to the event of 1627/28.

## A possible family

In documents of the 1640s Kuzma Karpovich Anfilogov appears. The same patronymic, the rare surname and Oryol make it a plausible hypothesis that Mark and Kuzma were brothers. There is as yet no direct formula "brothers" or a shared father in a single entry, so the Record does not assert the kinship.

- actual possession of all 100 chetverti;
- any specific campaign or place of assembly in 1627/28;
- the name of the person who carried out the enrolment;
- that he and Kuzma Karpovich were brothers.
