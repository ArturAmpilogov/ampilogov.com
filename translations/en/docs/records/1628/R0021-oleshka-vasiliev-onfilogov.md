# R0021 — Oleshka Vasilyev syn Onfilogov, Novik of 1621/22

## Original spelling in the publication

> Новики 130-го году. […] По 200 чети. Олешка Васильев сын Онфилогов.

> *Translation:* Noviki of the year [7]130. […] At 200 chetverti each. Oleshka, son of Vasily, Onfilogov.

## Extracted facts

- Oleshka is a historical diminutive form of the name Aleksei;
- patronymic: Vasilyev syn ("son of Vasily");
- surname form: Onfilogov;
- town of registration: Oryol;
- explicitly named among the **noviki of 7130**, that is, of 1621/22;
- pomestye oklad: **200 chetverti**;
- entered in the list on fol. 128.

## What "novik" means

Here a novik is not simply a young man, nor a newcomer to the current campaign. He is a serviceman first enrolled in pomestye service in the year stated and assigned a fixed oklad. So by the time the list of 1627/28 was drawn up, Aleksei had already been registered for about six years as an enrolled novik of 1621/22.

The document does not give his age; the year of birth is established from other sources. 200 chetverti is the reckoned norm of the oklad; actual landholding is checked against pomestye and cadastral books.

## Who enrolled him and where the men were assembled

The available text does not name the person who carried out the original enrolment of 7130. The list of 7136 merely preserves the result of the earlier enrolment. It indicates no separate campaign regiment, direction of march or new place of assembly.

Aleksei is also present in the 1626 muster roll compiled from the muster at Mtsensk. This confirms that he actually appeared at that muster, but the list of 1627/28 cannot automatically be taken as a register of the same Mtsensk assembly.

## The biography continues

Oleshka Vasilyev syn Onfilogov is named twice in the transcription of the 1626 list — on fols. 105 and 149. The name index of the estimate book of 1641/42 places Aleksei Vasilyev Anfilogov on fol. 375. The full match of name, patronymic and town supports the identification, but the manuscript line of 1641/42 has yet to be located.

## What it does not prove

- Aleksei's exact age and year of birth;
- the name of the official who enrolled him in 1621/22;
- participation in any particular campaign;
- actual possession of all 200 chetverti;
- his family, or kinship with Ivan Vasilyevich without a direct statement of kinship.
