# R0010 — Yermol Fomin syn Anpilogov, 1659

## Original spelling

> Ермол Фомин с(ы)нъ Анпилогов.

> *Translation:* Yermol, son of Foma, Anpilogov.

## Full transcription

Ермол Фомин сын Анпилогов.

*Translation:* Yermol, son of Foma, Anpilogov.

The abbreviation `с(ы)нъ` is expanded as «сын» ("son"). The given name and surname are rendered without modern normalisation.

## Extracted facts

- given name in the document: Yermol;
- patronymic in its 17th-century form: Fomin syn ("son of Foma");
- surname form: Anpilogov;
- place in the list: under the rubric «Кромченин» ("Man of Kromy");
- document: the muster list of the regiment of the okolnichy Prince G. G. Romodanovsky, 1659.

## What it proves

- Yermol Fomin syn Anpilogov is named on a separate line in the muster list of 1659;
- a separate rubric before the line directly links him with Kromy;
- the document records the surname form `Анпилогов`.

## What it does not prove

- Yermol's kinship with the Oryol Yermak of the late 16th century or other bearers of a similar name;
- the size of his pomestye (service estate), his oklad or his family;
- his later place of service.

## Reading notes

- the line has been checked against the full verso and the enlarged fragment;
- the rubric «Белевцы» ("Men of Belyov") belongs to the previous entry; before Yermol stands a new rubric, «Кромченин»;
- the modern form "Yermol Fomich" is used only in the index of people.
