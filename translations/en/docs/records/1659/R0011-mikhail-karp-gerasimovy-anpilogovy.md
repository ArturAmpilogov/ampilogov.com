# R0011 — Mikhaylo and Karp, Garasimovy deti Anpilogovy, 1659

## Original spelling

> Михайло да Карпъ Гарасимовы дети Анпилоговы.

> *Translation:* Mikhaylo and Karp, children of Garasim, Anpilogov.

## Full transcription

Михайло да Карп, Гарасимовы дети Анпилоговы.

*Translation:* Mikhaylo and Karp, children of Garasim, Anpilogov.

Two people are named in one self-contained name unit. The formula
`Гарасимовы дети` ("Garasim's children") directly means that Mikhaylo and Karp are sons of Garasim;
`Анпилоговы` gives the brothers' shared surname form.

## Extracted facts

- Mikhaylo and Karp are named in one entry;
- both are directly called children of Garasim;
- surname form in the document: Анпилоговы (Anpilogovs);
- the entry is in the Stary Oskol part of the list;
- document: the muster list of the regiment of the okolnichy Prince G. G. Romodanovsky,
  1659.

## What it proves

- Mikhail and Karp are named together as sons of Gerasim;
- in 1659 the brothers are recorded in the form `Анпилоговы`;
- a year after the Oskol list of 1658 the same pair of names with the same patronymic
  appears again in the source.

## What it does not prove

- that their father is Garasim Ivanov syn Onpilogov from the list of 1655;
- that the entry of 1658 and the entry of 1659 undoubtedly refer to the same
  people, although the agreement of two names, the patronymic, the place and adjacent years makes this
  identification very likely;
- the size of the brothers' pomestye, their oklad or the make-up of their household.
