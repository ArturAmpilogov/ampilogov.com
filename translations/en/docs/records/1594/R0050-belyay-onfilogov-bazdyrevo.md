# R0050 — Belyay Vasilyevich Onfilogov in Bazdyrevo, 1594/1595

## Original entry

> За Бѣляемъ за Васильевымъ сыномъ Онфилогова въ той же дер. въ Баздыревѣ, на Сычовѣ колодезѣ, подъ Миновымъ лѣсомъ, на его жеребью: дв. помѣщиковъ, да дв. крестьянской пустъ; пашни паханые добр. земли 9 чети, да дикого поля 51 четь въ полѣ, а въ дву потому жъ, сѣна 115 коп.

> *Translation:* Held by Belyay, son of Vasily, Onfilogov, in the same hamlet of Bazdyrevo, on the Sychov spring, below the Minov forest, on his share: a landholder's household, and an empty peasant household; ploughed land of good soil 9 chetverti, and wild field 51 chetverti in a field, and in the other two likewise; hay 115 kopny.

## Extracted facts

- name: **Belyay Vasilyevich Onfilogov**;
- spelling of the surname: `Онфилогова`;
- place: Bazdyrevo on the Sychov spring below the Minov forest;
- one landholder's household;
- one empty peasant household;
- 9 chetverti of ploughed land of good soil;
- 51 chetverti of wild field in each field of the three-field system;
- a hay meadow of 115 kopny.

An empty peasant household means that the household plot was counted, but there was no living peasant household on it at the time of the survey. The reason for its abandonment is not stated.

## Who compiled the survey

The lands were measured by the scribe **Dementy Yakovlev** and the clerk **Leonty Sofonov**. Their task was to check pomestye grants, households and lands, not to review the landholders as soldiers.

## Service status

Belyay was a landholder from among the Oryol deti boyarskie. The general heading of the cadastral book lists regimental, stanitsa, samopal and siege men, as well as widows and nedorosli (sons not yet of service age). But Belyay's individual entry does not name a particular service. So he cannot automatically be recorded as a stanitsa or regimental man.

## The form of the surname within one document

On this page Belyay is `Онфилогов`, while Osip and the sons of Mikula are `Анфилоговы`. On the following pages `Анпилогов` occurs. The different forms appear in one cadastral book, in one district and partly in one hamlet. This is strong evidence of scribal and spoken variation in the initial vowel and in the consonants `ф/п`.

However, one hamlet and neighbouring holdings do not yet prove any particular kinship between Belyay and Osip or the sons of Mikula.

## Was Belyay a novik, and where did he go

A cadastral book is not a desyatnya (service register). It does not call Belyay a novik (newly enrolled serviceman), does not describe his horse and weapons, and gives neither the army assembly nor the direction of a campaign. Holding a pomestye confirms a service milieu, but not a specific campaign.

## The local milieu

In Bazdyrevo the Anfilogovs, Nemtsovs, Krivolapovs and Bazdyrevs held land side by side; on the sovereign's land stood a pogost (churchyard settlement) with the church of Nicholas the Wonderworker. This is not an isolated, remote holding but a dense cluster of small servicemen's shares.

## What it proves

- Belyay's full name and the form `Онфилогов`;
- the presence of a landholder's household and an empty peasant household;
- the composition of his land share in 1594/1595;
- that he belonged to the Oryol pomestye milieu.

## What it does not prove

- kinship with the neighbouring Anfilogovs;
- the reason the peasant household was abandoned;
- his exact military category, novik status or campaign;
- that the surveyed area matched his service oklad.
