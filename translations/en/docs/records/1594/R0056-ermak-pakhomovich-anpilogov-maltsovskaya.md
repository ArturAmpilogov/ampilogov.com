# R0056 — Ermak Pakhomovich Anpilogov in Maltsovskaya, 1594/1595

## Full entry

> За Ермакомъ за Пахомовымъ сыномъ Анпилогова въ той же дер. въ Малцовской, на р. на Нугри, на его жеребью: дв. помѣщиковъ да 2 дв. крестьянскихъ пусты; пашни паханые добр. земли 7 чети, да дикого поля 19 чети въ полѣ, а въ дву потому жъ, сѣна 40 коп.

> *Translation:* Held by Ermak, son of Pakhom, Anpilogov, in the same hamlet of Maltsovskaya, on the river Nugr, on his share: a landholder's household and 2 peasant households empty; ploughed land of good soil 7 chetverti, and wild field 19 chetverti in a field, and in the other two likewise; hay 40 kopny.

## The easternmost entry of the group

Ermak Pakhomovich is the only one of the nine holders of 1594/95 recorded not in Korchakov stan but in the neighbouring **Nugorsk** (or Nougorsk) stan. His share lay in the hamlet of Maltsovskaya on the river Nugr. This entry extends the Oryol cluster beyond Bazdyrevo, Belaya, Sofonova and the Radomlya, while still keeping it within one district.

L. M. Savyolov's later reference work assigned all nine to Korchakov stan. The cadastral book is more precise than the reference work: the page heading and the sequence of the survey place Ermak in Nugorsk stan.

## Households and holding

Recorded under Ermak:

- one landholder's household;
- two empty peasant households;
- 7 chetverti of ploughed land of good soil;
- 19 chetverti of wild field in each field of the three-field system;
- 40 kopny of hay.

Two empty households are not two living families. The household plots and traces of farming continued to be counted, but there were no peasants there at the time of the survey. The book does not say whether the former inhabitants had died, left or been moved.

## Name and spelling of the surname

`Пахомов сын` means the son of a man named Pakhom; the modern rendering is **Ermak Pakhomovich Anpilogov**. At the end of the 16th century `Ермак` (Ermak) could be an independent secular name, not an indication of an occupation or a Cossack nickname.

The surname is printed with `Анп-`: `сыномъ Анпилогова`. The same form occurs for Ivan Nechayevich, whereas other holders in this book are called Onfilogovs or Anfilogovs. The similarity of the rare surname stem and the shared Oryol context allow them to be treated as one research cluster, but Ermak's entry establishes no specific kinship with any of them.

## Who compiled the survey

The land was described by the scribe **Dementy Yakovlev** and the clerk **Leonty Sofonov**. They recorded the existing farm and the areas of land for pomestye and tax accounting. The document names no separate person who inspected Ermak himself as a soldier.

## Service, novik status and campaigns

Ermak was a landholder of Oryol district and belonged to the service milieu of the deti boyarskie. His individual category, however, is not named: it is impossible to choose between regimental, stanitsa, samopal and siege service.

He is not marked as a novik. Having his own household and land already under the plough shows an established holding, but does not tell how many years Ermak had served. The cadastral book does not name his horse, weapons, regiment, the voivode at a muster or an assembly point.

## What it proves

- that Ermak held a share in Maltsovskaya;
- his patronymic, the surname form `Анпилогова` and Nugorsk stan;
- the presence of a landholder's household and two empty peasant households;
- the size of the ploughland, wild field and hay meadow.

## What it does not prove

- any specific kinship with the Korchakov holders;
- the reason the peasant households were abandoned;
- novik status or the exact kind of service;
- participation in a campaign or in Cossack service.
