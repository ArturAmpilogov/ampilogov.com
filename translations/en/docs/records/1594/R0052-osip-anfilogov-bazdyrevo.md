# R0052 — Osip Vasilyevich Anfilogov Receives Krivolapov's Former Pomestye, 1594/1595

## Original entry

> За Осипомъ за Васильевымъ сыномъ Анфилогова, что было въ помѣстьѣ за Онохою за Дѣевымъ сыномъ Криволапова въ той же дер. въ Баздыревѣ, на Сычовѣ колодезѣ, подъ Миновымъ лѣсомъ, на его жеребью: дв. помѣщиковъ; пашни паханые добр. земли 5 чети, да дикого поля 19 чети въ полѣ, а въ дву потому жъ, сѣна 70 коп.

> *Translation:* Held by Osip, son of Vasily, Anfilogov — which had been the pomestye of Onokha, son of Dey, Krivolapov — in the same hamlet of Bazdyrevo, on the Sychov spring, below the Minov forest, on his share: a landholder's household; ploughed land of good soil 5 chetverti, and wild field 19 chetverti in a field, and in the other two likewise; hay 70 kopny.

## Extracted facts

- present holder: **Osip Vasilyevich Anfilogov**;
- former holder: **Onokha, son of Dey, Krivolapov**;
- one landholder's household;
- 5 chetverti of ploughed land of good soil;
- 19 chetverti of wild field in each field of the three-field system;
- a hay meadow of 70 kopny;
- place: Bazdyrevo on the Sychov spring below the Minov forest.

## What the former holding means

The phrase `что было в поместье за Онохою` ("which had been the pomestye of Onokha") records a change of holder of the pomestye grant. It does not explain the reason. Onokha may have died, lost the grant, moved elsewhere or been removed from the holding for some other reason. Without a further document the pomestye cannot be called escheated.

Onokha's brother, Lyova, son of Dey, Krivolapov, continued to hold a separate share in the same hamlet. This shows that the transfer concerned a particular grant, not necessarily the whole Krivolapov family.

## Who described the pomestye

The scribe **Dementy Yakovlev** and the clerk **Leonty Sofonov** checked the holdings of Korchakov stan. Naming the former holder made it possible to link Osip's new entry with earlier pomestye books and to confirm the origin of the grant.

## Possible kinship with Belyay

Osip and Belyay are both Vasilyevichi (sons of a Vasily), both bear variants of the same rare surname and both hold shares in Bazdyrevo on the same page. This makes them strong candidates for brothers. But the source does not call them brothers and does not say that their fathers named Vasily are the same person. So the link should for now be kept as a hypothesis.

## Service status, novik status and campaigns

Osip was an Oryol landholder from among the deti boyarskie. Receiving a former grant may have accompanied verstanie (enrolment) or a reallocation for service, but the entry itself does not say so. Osip is not called a novik.

This is a land entry, not a muster. It contains no information on weapons, horse, fitness, the army's assembly point or the direction of a campaign.

## What it proves

- Osip's full name;
- possession of a particular share in Bazdyrevo;
- the transfer of the grant from Onokha Krivolapov;
- the composition of the lands and hay meadow in 1594/1595.

## What it does not prove

- the reason for the transfer of the pomestye;
- that Osip and Belyay were brothers;
- novik status;
- any particular military service or campaign;
- kinship with the Anfilogov sons of Mikula.
