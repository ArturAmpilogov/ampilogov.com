# R0049 — Kondraty Onanyin Holds His Grandfather Anpilog's Share, 1594/1595

## Full entry

> За Кондрашкомъ за Вавиловымъ сыномъ Онаньина, что было за дѣдомъ его за Анпилогомъ (sic) за Ивановымъ сыномъ Онаньина въ тойже дер. на Государнинѣ селищѣ, на р. на Орлѣ, подъ Корчаковымъ лѣсомъ, на его жеребью: дв. помѣщиковъ; пашни паханые добр. земли 7 четьи да дикого поля 44 чети въ полѣ, а въ дву потомужъ, сѣна 110 коп.

> *Translation:* Held by Kondrashko, son of Vavila, Onanyin — which had been held by his grandfather Anpilog (sic), son of Ivan, Onanyin — in the same hamlet on the Gosudarnino selishche, on the river Orel, below the Korchakov forest, on his share: a landholder's household; ploughed land of good soil 7 chetverti and wild field 44 chetverti in a field, and in the other two likewise; hay 110 kopny.

## Holder and succession

In 1594/1595 the zherebye (land share) is recorded under **Kondrashko, son of Vavila, Onanyin**, that is, Kondraty Vavilovich Onanyin. The book states directly that the holding had previously belonged to his grandfather **Anpilog Ivanovich Onanyin**.

The formula establishes a reliable line:

`Анпилог Иванович → его сын Вавила → Кондратий Вавилович`.

Vavila is not named as a holder in his own right and is known here only through his son's patronymic. It is not proven that the share passed directly from grandfather to grandson: there may have been temporary holders or an administrative re-registration in between.

## Composition of the share

- one landholder's household;
- 7 chetverti of ploughed land of good soil;
- 44 chetverti of wild field in one field, «а в двух потому же» ("and in the other two likewise");
- a hay meadow of 110 kopny.

Under the three-field formula the nominal area of the entry is three equal fields, but this is not Kondraty's cash or land oklad (service entitlement). The surveyed area and the service oklad are different measures.

## Who described it and why

The scribe **Dementy Yakovlev** and the clerk **Leonty Sofonov** carried out a general survey of the pomestye lands of Oryol district. They measured ploughland and wild field, counted households and hay meadows, and by naming the former holder linked the new entry to earlier grants.

## Service context

Kondraty was a landholder and an Oryol syn boyarsky (one of the deti boyarskie). Korchakov stan included the lands of regimental, stanitsa, samopal and siege servicemen. But the book does not say which group Kondraty belonged to.

This is not a muster of fighting men. Kondraty is not called a novik; horse, weapons, armour, assembly point and campaign direction are absent.

## Why the entry matters for the surname research

Kondraty himself is not an Anpilogov but an Onanyin. The value of the entry is that it preserved his grandfather's name `Анпилог` in the same Korchakov stan where, somewhat later, the surnames `Онфилогов`, `Анфилогов` and `Анпилогов` are recorded. This is strong evidence that the name was current locally, but not of kinship between the two families.

## What it proves

- that Kondraty held the share in 1594/1595;
- his patronymic and surname;
- that Anpilog Ivanovich was his grandfather;
- the composition of the land entry at the date of the survey.

## What it does not prove

- direct transfer without intermediate holders;
- that the Onanyins belonged to the Anpilogov family;
- the precise kind of military service;
- that Kondraty or Anpilog took part in a campaign.
