# Bazdyrevo / Bozdyrevo

## Role in the Research

Bazdyrevo in Korchakov stan (subdistrict) of Oryol district is one of the key place names of the Oryol cluster. Anfilogovs are linked to it in the pistsovye knigi (cadastral surveys) and service records of the late 16th and 17th centuries.

In his personal skazka (written statement) of 1700, Akim Ivanovich **Anpilogov** explicitly called himself an Oryol pomeshchik (service landholder) of Bozdyrevo and described the forcible seizure of his estate. In the same original document, 30 chetverti in Shchigry in Livny district are recorded under his grandson Tit. This creates a proved geographical bridge to Livny district, but does not yet connect all the earlier Bazdyrevo families with Akim.

## Spelling Variants

- Баздырево (Bazdyrevo);
- Боздырево (Bozdyrevo);
- Боздорево (Bozdorevo) in a secondary transcription.

All forms must be checked in archival searches.
