# Samara

## Status of the Place

FamilySearch assigns the parish register page found to Samara and wrongly
derives the modern chain "Samara, Kuybyshev Oblast, Russia, Soviet Union" for
an event of 1907. This line is kept as an index hint but is not accepted as the
literal historical place.

In the image the parents and godparents are described with their social estate
and place of residence. The handwriting in the relevant line has not yet been
fully read, so the exact parish and administrative attribution remains in the
verification queue.
