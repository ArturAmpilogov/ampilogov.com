# Geography and Further Lines of Search

## Documented Clusters before the 18th Century

- Uglich district: Priluki;
- Oryol district: Bazdyrevo/Bozdyrevo, Belaya, Sofonovo, Radomlya, Maltsovskaya;
- Kursk;
- Oboyan;
- Livny district: Dmitrievskoye, also called Shchigry.

## Documented Migrations and New Clusters of the 19th–20th Centuries

- Northern Taurida: Tymoshivka, Troitskoye and other parishes of Melitopol district;
- Kherson governorate: Privolnoye, Yelanets, Novy Bug, Nikolayev and Odessa;
- Orenburg Line: Karmalka, Orenburg and Orsk;
- Siberia: Tomsk and Yeniseysk governorates, including Beya volost and Minusinsk okrug;
- Ufa governorate;
- Sunzha Line: the family of Parfyon Anpilov assigned for resettlement in 1847;
- Venyov district of Tula governorate: the surname Anpilogov among a group of 17 households from Fatezh that in 1903 formed the new village of Khokhly.

Each point and route is accompanied by a card for the person or family household, a date, the type of document, and the stage of migration: petition, permission, assignment, arrival, or residence already confirmed at the new place.

## Crimean Sources

The following are available for finding further branches of the surname:

- the digital collection of GARK (State Archive of the Republic of Crimea) parish registers for 1783–1925;
- the FamilySearch collection "Ukraine, Crimea, Church Books, 1806–1926";
- the pamyatnye knizhki (official yearbooks) of Taurida governorate;
- revision (census) lists and household lists for individual districts.

Variants of the surname must be checked in Russian, in Ukrainian and in Latin-script indexing.

## Archival Directions for Further Search

- the north of mainland Taurida and the neighbouring parts of Kherson governorate;
- Yekaterinoslav governorate and Aleksandrovsk district;
- the Kursk, Oryol, Belgorod and Voronezh zones where the surname was anciently spread;
- the present-day archives of Zaporizhzhia, Kherson, Dnipropetrovsk, Kursk and Oryol oblasts, because the boundaries of archival custody do not coincide with the historical boundaries.
