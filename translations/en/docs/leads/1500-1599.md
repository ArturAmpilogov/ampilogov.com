# Early Leads of the 16th Century

## Two False Finds Ruled Out

The old working table contained rows on **Semyon Anfilogov** from the Novgorod
land and **Timofey Fyodorov syn Anfilogov** (Timofey, son of Fyodor, Anfilogov) from
the Obonezh pyatina (one of the five districts of the Novgorod land). A second check
traced the source of the error: page 465 of N. M. Tupikov's dictionary carries the
entry **Апрѣлевъ** (Aprelev), and the abbreviation `А-в` stands for the surname
**Апрелев** (Aprelev). The Timofey of 1565 and the Semyon of 1595 named there are
Aprelevs, not Anfilogovs.

V. A. Nikonov's entry "Анфилогов" (Anfilogov) on pages 135–136 of the 1973 offprint
gives the etymology of the surname but does not name these people. Both erroneous
rows have been removed from the list of archival leads to order.

Sources: [N. M. Tupikov, «Словарь древнерусских личных собственных имён» (Dictionary of Old Russian Personal Names),
p. 465 of the scan](https://www.phantastike.com/history/tupikov_slovar_drevrus_licsob_imen/pdf/),
[V. A. Nikonov, «Опыт словаря русских фамилий.
III» (An Experimental Dictionary of Russian Surnames. III)](https://ruslang.ru/doc/etymology/1973/12-nikonov.pdf).

## Result of the Repeat Search

No new verifiable mentions of the surname have been found for the 14th–15th
centuries or for the 16th century before 1536/37. The alphabetical sections of
Tupikov's dictionary and the full text of S. B. Veselovsky's «Ономастикон»
(Onomasticon) were checked in addition. The search hits relate either to the
personal name Amphilochius (Амфилохий), or to a different surname, Anfilov, or to
late family trees with no reference to a medieval document.

The lower limit for the proven family form therefore stays where it was: **Tonkoy
Onfilogov, 1536/37**. The absence of an earlier find in the open catalogues
does not prove that no such records existed.

## Where the Original of the 1536/37 Record Is Kept

S. M. Kashtanov's publication made it possible to pin down the archival location:
**РНБ (National Library of Russia), M. P. Pogodin collection, No. 1846, fol. 65v.**
The abbreviation `Погод. 1846` is the manuscript number, not the year 1846. In the
article the line about Tonkoy Onfilogov is printed on page 43 as part of document No. 3.

The РНБ's open catalogue of digitised manuscripts was checked on 20 August 2026:
manuscript No. 1846 is not in it. The published text has therefore been kept as a
source card rather than a `Record`. A full record requires ordering a digital copy
of **folio 65v** from the РНБ and making a separate excerpt and a literal
transcription from it.

Links: [Kashtanov's
publication](https://azbyka.ru/otechnik/assets/uploads/zor/%D0%97%D0%9E%D0%A0_40.pdf),
[РНБ digital library of manuscripts](https://nlr.ru/manuscripts/fondy/digital-library),
[contacts of the РНБ Manuscripts Department](https://nlr.ru/manuscripts).

## Published Corpora Checked

Using the available full-text layer, the main variants of the surname and the
broad roots `-пилог-` / `-филог-` were searched in the following editions:

- «Тысячная книга 1550 г. и Дворовая тетрадь 50-х годов XVI в.» (The Thousand Book of 1550 and the Court Register of the 1550s);
- the cadastral books (pistsovye knigi) of the Obonezh pyatina for 1496 and 1563;
- «Писцовые книги XVI века» (Cadastral Books of the 16th Century), division 1;
- the southern volume of «Писцовые книги Московского государства» (Cadastral Books
  of the Muscovite State) covering the Kaluga, Oryol and Tula lands.

No matches were found in the recognised text of these publications. The next
stage of the search covers unpublished files and pages without reliable OCR.
