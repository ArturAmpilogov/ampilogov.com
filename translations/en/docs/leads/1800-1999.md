# Unverified Finds, 1800–1999

This page holds indexes and derived cards for which no saved photocopy exists yet. They are not treated as facts of the book and do not receive a `P####`.

## Timoshevka, Births of 1845–1852

Three records were viewed on FamilySearch on 19 August 2026. For each, a
screenshot of the visible viewer frame and a machine JSON card were saved. The
screenshots are not yet archival master copies, and the literal transcription needs
a second palaeographic check, so permanent `R####` and `P####` IDs have not been
assigned yet.

- 18 October 1845 — Andrey Anpilogov; father Yelisey Pavlov Anpilogov, mother
  Paraskeva Grigoryeva. [FamilySearch frame](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CS9Y-XQPL-D),
  index `6XCD-NLV9`, [JSON](../../data/genealogy/sources/familysearch/3Q9M-CS9Y-XQPL-D.json),
  [saved screenshot](../evidence/familysearch/timoshovka/andrei-anpilogov-1845-page.jpg).
- 3 July 1849 — Andrey; the submitted reading of the surname is `Емпалоговъ` (Empalogov), the index
  reading `Еппалогов` (Eppalogov); father in the index Stefan Ivanov Eppalogov, mother Mariya
  Yevfimova. [FamilySearch frame](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSSQ-3WGP-9),
  index `6XC6-M4PX`, [JSON](../../data/genealogy/sources/familysearch/3Q9M-CSSQ-3WGP-9.json),
  [saved screenshot](../evidence/familysearch/timoshovka/andrei-eppalogov-1849-page.jpg).
- 4 July 1852 — Andrey Anpilogov; father in the index Aleksiy Pavlov Aknilogov (Акнилогов),
  mother Paraskeva Georgiyeva. The father's surname in the original remains disputed.
  [FamilySearch index](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:6XCD-5TD5),
  [original frame](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSSQ-37HK-W),
  [JSON](../../data/genealogy/sources/familysearch/3Q9M-CSSQ-37HK-W.json),
  [saved screenshot](../evidence/familysearch/timoshovka/andrei-anpilogov-1852-page.jpg).

These three Andreys are treated as three separate mentions. The coincidence of
name and place gives no grounds for merging them into one person; a name could be
reused after the death of an earlier child, but for now that is only a line of
inquiry.

## Timoshevka, Re-transcription of the Images

The images have been saved and given JSON cards, but the following readings remain
provisional:

- the name of the daughter of Leonty Andreyevich and Natalya Ivanovna, born 8 January 1922;
- the full patronymic of Vladimir, father of Anna Vladimirovna (20 March 1922);
- the name «Мария» (Mariya) in the record of the daughter of Arseny Andreyev of 7 April 1922;
- the surname of Anastasiya in the marriage with Andrey Andreyevich of 24 October 1899;
- the reading «Илья Ампилов» (Ilya Ampilov) on image 7 of film 007817719.

Until they are re-read, these values are marked `transcription-review` or
`unverified-reading` and are not used for automatic merging of families.

## Troitskoye, Melitopol District

Derived Familio cards point to:

- Ivan Yefremovich Anpilogov, born about 1791;
- Stepan Ivanovich Anpilogov;
- Akim Stepanovich Ampilogov;
- children of the family born in Troitskoye in the mid-19th century.

Links: [Ivan Yefremovich](https://familio.org/persons/4aad7897-8068-4248-aeb3-d9c0a4fcc17c), [Stepan Ivanovich](https://familio.org/persons/1f4cf9fe-f544-48d0-add4-796d34513634), [Akim Stepanovich](https://familio.org/persons/c49acc94-6e3e-4f7c-b9d7-d42cca37cf53).

The original parish register entries need to be found and the full frames saved; only after that should people and facts be created.
