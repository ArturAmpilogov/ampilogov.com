# Unverified Finds, 1600–1699

This page collects name indexes to RGADA files for which the project does not yet have
photocopies or a literal transcription. An index helps to find a file but does not
replace the archival folio: the names, spellings of the surname, oklady (service entitlements) and
presumed connections listed below are not treated as facts of the book. An exact reading from a full
publication with a folio number may be saved as a source card with the status
`index-only`; a full `Record` appears only after checking an image of the
original or a full scholarly publication of the document.

## Kursk Customs Books, 1619 and 1624

A scholarly edition of the Kursk customs books made it possible to replace two brief entries
of the alphabetical index with exact archival locations and the full texts of the transactions.

- **22 June 1619:** Tereshka Ivanov, a peasant of **Semyon Anpilogov**,
  registered the purchase from Yesya Stepanov of Kursk of a five-year-old dun mare
  for five roubles. Archival location: **RGADA, f. 210, Razryad bundles, bundle 1, pt. 1,
  No. 10, fols. 62–62v.** The editors noted separately the case irregularity of the form
  `Анпилогов` (Anpilogov) and proposed the reading `Анпилогова?` (Anpilogova?, genitive).
- **21 May 1624:** **Ivan Onpilogov** of Kursk bought a dark bay gelding from the peasant Anton
  Alekseyev for two roubles. Archival location: **RGADA,
  f. 210, Money Desk (Denezhny stol), book 82, fol. 78v.**

For both lines the pages of the scholarly publication, large excerpts and
the full published transcriptions have been saved. These are separate source cards, but not yet
full `Record`s: the project has no images of the handwritten folios.

## Oryol, 1620/21: the Full Name Index

The full publication of the index to the file **RGADA, f. 210, op. 14, d. 502** names
three people and gives exact folios:

| Folio | Published reading |
|---|---|
| 67 | Василей Дмитреев Анпилогов (Vasiley, son of Dmitry, Anpilogov) |
| 94 | Олексей Васильев Анфилохов (Oleksey, son of Vasily, Anfilokhov) |
| 109 | Иван Васильев Анфилохов (Ivan, son of Vasily, Anfilokhov) |

This check corrects the earlier machine selection. The names «Марк» (Mark) and «Илюшка» (Ilyushka) are absent from
the full index to this file; the file of 1620/21 cannot be linked with
hypotheses H-04 and H-05.

An open collection of photocopies of the file has been found, and fols. 67, 94, 94v and 109 have been saved
locally together with checksums. The full set of 409 frames made it possible to
localise Aleksey Vasilyevich's entry on fol. 94v and Ivan Vasilyevich's entry
in the upper part of fol. 109; the beginning of Vasily Dmitriyevich's entry was found at the bottom of fol. 67.
Exact crops have been saved for all three places. Until a full literal
transcription of the service formulas is made, the entries remain source cards, not
`Records`. The working files and the provenance of the copies are described in
[`public/archive/evidence/rgada/f210-op14-d502`](../evidence/rgada/f210-op14-d502/README.md).
In the fragments found so far a direct family formula cannot yet be read with confidence:
the identical patronymic of Aleksey and Ivan makes them possible brothers, but
does not prove it by itself. In the register of 1625 it is Aleksey and
Grigory who are explicitly called brothers — «Васильевы дети Анфилогова» ("Vasily's children Anfilogov"); Ivan is recorded separately. Vasily
Dmitriyevich also remains a separate person, and the name of his father Dmitry is known
only from the patronymic.

## Oryol, 1627/28: the Full Text Has Been Published

A scholarly publication of the "original" Oryol list makes it possible to establish the full
archival location: **RGADA, f. 210, op. 14, d. 22**. Four
separate lines on four folios have been found in the file:

| Folio | Published reading | Oklad |
|---|---|---:|
| 123 | Васка Дмитреев сын Онпилогов (Vaska, son of Dmitry, Onpilogov) | 200 chetverti |
| 125 | Ивашко Васильев сын Онфилогов (Ivashko, son of Vasily, Onfilogov) | 150 chetverti |
| 128 | Олешка Васильев сын Онфилогов (Oleshka, son of Vasily, Onfilogov) | 200 chetverti |
| 133 | Марка Карпов сын Онфилохов (Marka, son of Karp, Onfilokhov) | 100 chetverti |

This is no longer a brief surname index but a published transcription of the source.
A separate card with the literal reading has been created for each line. Photocopies of
folios 123, 125, 128 and 133 are still needed: without them the handwriting,
the context of the line and the peculiarities of the original cannot be checked. An oklad in chetverti here means
the reckoned standard of land provision, not the proven size of a pomestye actually
received and ploughed.

### Why These Files Matter

- The index of 1620/21 gives the exact folios of Vasily Dmitriyevich and of Aleksey and
  Ivan Vasilyevich. It does not confirm the earlier machine matches «Марк» (Mark) and
  «Илюшка» (Ilyushka); these names have been removed from the description of the file.
- The exact line-by-line selection for the file of 1642 confirms seven lines with the forms
  `Анпилов` (Anpilov) and `Анфилогов` (Anfilogov). A separate transcription of the file itself and the original of folio
  385v add an eighth line: `Ермак Павлов сын Онпилогов на мерине с
  рогатиною` (Ermak, son of Pavel, Onpilogov, on a gelding with a boar-spear). It has been checked against the image and entered as `R0012`.
- The forms `Анпилогов` (Anpilogov) and `Онпилогов` (Onpilogov), which really do occur in other
  Oryol lists, must not be carried over into the file of 1642 without checking the
  folios themselves. The exception now is the form `Онпилогов` in Ermak's line on
  fol. 385v: it is visible in the original.
- Ofonko Osipovich of 1628/29 and Ofonasey Osipovich of 1641/42 are confirmed
  as one person by the exact match of name, patronymic, the rare surname and Oryol.

For the earliest file an independent confirmation has been found. The siege list of
Belgorod of 1633 is known to researchers as **RGADA, f. 210, op. 12,

match remains a lead: it is not known exactly whom the index read as
Anpilogov.

## Negative Results

- No variants of the surname were found in the published alphabetical index to the Yelets desyatni and payment books
  of 1604–1621.
- In the index to the file of 1629 the surname is noted only in the Oryol section out of
  seven towns. This is a useful boundary of the material viewed, but not
  proof that the family is absent from all other documents of these districts.
- No Oryol group has yet been found earlier than the cadastral book (pistsovaya kniga) of 1594/95.
  Since Oryol was founded in 1566, the preceding generations should also be sought
  in the districts from which servicemen were transferred to the new town.

2. Ask the compiler of the index for the call number and folio of Fyodor Anpilogov,
   1605–1618.

   localise the entries, make exact crops and a full transcription.

   the Oryol list of 1627/28.
5. RGADA, f. 210, op. 4, d. 260, fols. 374v, 375, 388, 392v, 393v,
   402 and 403v — Oryol, 1642.

   Anpilov and his two sons, Oskol — Tsarev-Alekseyev, 1646/47–1648.
7. RGADA, f. 210, op. 21, d. 2099 and d. 1952 — two fragments of a presumed
   Oryol book of the mid-17th century; establish the person and folio of the surname
   entry.
8. RGADA, f. 210, op. 9, d. 1101, pt. 4 — establish the folio of Zinov
   Onpilogov and order a good-quality image of the whole line.
9. RGADA, f. 210, Razryad bundles, bundle 1, pt. 1, No. 10, fols. 62–62v — the transaction
   of Semyon Anpilogov's peasant, 1619; f. 210, Money Desk, book 82,
   fol. 78v — Ivan Onpilogov, 1624.
10. RGADA, f. 210, op. 12, d. 7, fols. 84–146 — Kursk, 1630.

12. A better-quality copy of RGADA, f. 210, op. 12, pt. 1, d. 398, fol. 133 —
    final check of the Onpilogov brothers of 1658.

For each folio found, a full scan and a large excerpt must be saved,
a literal transcription made, the archival call number given, and only then a
separate `Record` created. If one folio has several separate lines, each
line gets its own card.
